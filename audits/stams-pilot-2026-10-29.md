# Stams document pilot, October 2026

Five Fragmentarium records from Stams, Stiftsarchiv, catalogued only by
document type and date, were researched on 29 October 2026 with
[prompts/document-brief.md](../prompts/document-brief.md): F-jlq7 (Frg. 191),
F-pqjj (Frg. 201), F-fc4z (Frg. 202), F-xyan (Frg. 200) and F-d3a1 (Frg. 199).
They hold seven documents representing five distinct acts; F-pqjj and F-fc4z
carry the same grant of arms. All five records are recorded as identified.

## Checks and corrections before publication

- A Codex review of the first results found that F-xyan reads 1545
  ("funfundvierzigisten"), not 1525 as first reported and as the catalogue
  gives. The coordinator's image check had accepted the agent's year. The
  opening's royal titles (Roman, Hungarian and Bohemian king) confirm 1545.
  The proposed ward, Veit von Wolkenstein, fell with the date; his son
  Christof remains a lead not read on the sheet. The same review found the
  `feastdate.py` feria bug fixed in PR #58.
- Three fresh readers then saw only the text-face images and neutral
  questions. Every disagreement was re-read at native resolution. This
  corrected: the extent of F-jlq7 doc. 1's dating clause (feast, year and
  mediator supplied from Chmel); F-jlq7 doc. 2's issuer (under tape, inferred);
  F-fc4z's day and month (uncertain on the sheet, supplied from Frank);
  F-pqjj's year (probable only); F-xyan's "Küniglicher Maiestat",
  "Pflegsun" and beneficiary surname (Wenig or Rennig); F-d3a1's dating-clause
  place (present, illegible) and street name (uncertain). These readers worked
  after the identification, so they are not first readings in the
  [CONVENTIONS](../CONVENTIONS.md) sense and are not preserved in `readings/`.
- Blind reader A's transcription of F-jlq7 doc. 1, made without Chmel, agrees
  with Chmel's printed text in wording and order across eight passages.

## Status rule

The pilot was first reported with three documents as partial (F-jlq7 doc. 2,
F-xyan, F-d3a1): in each the text is read but one element is lost or
uncertain (a burnt dating clause, one surname). A Fragmentarium editor,
asked whether documents read in full should count as identified, replied on
30 October 2026 approving the pilot. On that basis the rule in CONVENTIONS "Archival documents" now states that a single uncertain or lost
element is reported in the regest and does not make the result partial when
the rest of the text is read securely; partial is kept for documents whose
action or parties cannot be established or whose text is largely unread.
Under it all seven documents are identified. The uncertain elements are
stated in each report.
