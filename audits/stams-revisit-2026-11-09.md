# Stams fragments, second attempt on twelve open records, November 2026

A second attempt on twelve Stams records (collection C-ugo5) that the first
pass of October-November 2026 left partial or unidentified. The twelve were
chosen from the 98 open Stams rows as the text-bearing literary, legal,
medical and grammatical records with connected readings; archival documents,
music and records with almost no legible text were left for later.

## Process

1. No new first readings were made: each record's preserved image-only
   reading stands, and the new source-assisted readings are saved under the
   same reading names.
2. Research by six sessions (Claude Fable 5.2), two records each, with the
   research brief plus the earlier research report as context, asked to test
   the leads it left open and to reread weak passages on the image. Most of
   the gains came from rereading the images (a passage missed or misread in
   the first pass) rather than from the named leads.
3. The coordinator read one matched passage on the manuscript image and on
   the source's page image for each identification. A separate reader, given
   nine renamed crops from the three identified records and no source or
   report, transcribed them. On F-0w8s it marked most words uncertain and two of its four crops gave nothing usable; where it read words, they agree with the matched text
   (F-0w8s "An soli", "que non possunt", rubric "De Renu(n)ciacione"; F-d7a7
   "Suspensio ab officio", "De forma", numeral ccxxxij; F-53hg "intellegendo",
   "quasi in la[p]ide", numeral xxxiiij). The brief given to that reader used
   "ccxxxij" as its example of a numeral, so its agreement on that numeral
   carries less weight than the other words; the numeral was read the same
   way by the research reader before the print was found and by the
   coordinator.
4. F-53hg was sent back once to settle its layer: three manuscripts of the
   old Latin *Ad Glauconem* (Vendôme 109, Poitiers 184, BnF lat. 6837) agree
   with Stams at the four places where they differ from Gariopontus, and two
   *Passionarius* manuscripts (BnF NAL 695 on the page, BnF lat. 6951 by
   transcription) lack the section on fats, as the 1531 print does. The
   leaf's section number xxxiiii remains unexplained.

## Unavailable sources

- manuscripta.at returned 403 to scripts and to a browser (Klosterneuburg
  CCl 451 and Cod. 886, Admont manuscripts).
- Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin, Ms. germ. oct. 449 and 517: no digitisation
  listed in the Handschriftenportal; the SBB site could not be queried.
- `tools/gbwithin.py`: Google Books now answers the in-volume search with an
  HTML page, not JSON, so no in-volume searches were possible.

These are recorded as untested, not as negative results.
