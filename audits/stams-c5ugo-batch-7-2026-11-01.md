# Stams uncatalogued fragments, batch 7, November 2026

The seventh batch from the uncatalogued Stams fragments (collection C-ugo5;
title "x", shelfmark, material and dimensions only). Twenty-five records: the
thirteen remaining records the triage rated of medium promise (F-tnqw, with
its typed transcriptions, and the 44-piece F-lv0w again left for later) and
twelve low-promise records with the most legible text. Most are archival:
deeds, accounts, guardianship papers and letters.

## Process

1. Contact-sheet review for modern additions and orientation; 33 mask boxes
   on 18 records (typed transcriptions and analyses, pencil dates and host
   labels), F-agve's charter rotated. Tile packets from native images, one
   packet per record, at most 45 images. First readings by fresh model
   readers given only the prompt and tiles (`stams7-2026-11-01-image-only`),
   one reader per packet. The first F-g51l reader saw a typed line below the
   mask; that reading was set aside, the mask enlarged, and a fresh reader
   made the frozen reading. No reader stopped on the image limit; several
   left tiles the overviews showed blank unopened, and said so.
2. Research by fourteen sessions, one or two records each, with the document
   brief for the archival records. Research sessions see the native images
   unmasked, so reports say where a modern note agrees with a reading and
   where a reading would rest on one.
3. One batched CoMMA search after research, over the Latin queries of nine
   records: no lead (best margins were a Bible verse and chancery formula).
4. Checks. An independent checker reread passages of F-mrl7 against the 1518
   print on its page image and Harris's edition in snippets, and of F-13e8
   and F-027n on the images with their arithmetic and date conversions; the
   checkers had seen the reports, so these are rechecks. The coordinator read
   a matched F-mrl7 passage on the image. For the eighteen records with
   archival text, two fresh readers with no transcription read 117 shuffled
   neutral crops of dating clauses and names in three pools, and an
   adjudicator reread every crop on the image, reading it before opening the
   research reading.

## Corrections made by the checks

- F-13e8: both blind readers leaned to 1578 on two year crops; the
  coordinator reread them side by side and kept 1538 (the period's z-shaped
  3, and the text's own 1537 references). The 4v figures are Salgeber's
  deliveries against the 1537 yield, not a balance.
- F-027n: the husband is Martin Vischler ("Mart-/in"), not "Mar[x]"; the
  closing year has no century, supplied from the 1637 start date.
- F-mrl7: the §474 sentence does not agree with Harris; "word for word"
  withdrawn.
- F-agve: the year stays 154x against the first reading's 152x; the Pfleger's
  surname is "Ca[ss/sl]e", so Veit Castl is an inference.
- F-4568: the January day is "[..]igesim-", so the 30th or 31st is as possible
  as the modern note's 23rd.
- F-dqq1, F-5lit, F-kqf1, F-hui3, F-nciq, F-0dnc, F-geoq, F-hq07, F-otkb,
  F-rnqk: names and place readings queried or corrected (e.g. "Imst",
  "Pfaller", "Walthaur", "Crysten" now carry query marks; "Ragner" is
  "[...]ragne"); F-rnqk's decade word is damaged, so 1560 is marked
  uncertain; F-pmci's years were not in the crops and are given as
  unverified.
- F-uqw6: "et Tirolis" is not visible on the parchment and rests on the 1964
  typescript alone.
