# Stams fragments, second attempt on the last twenty-two open records, November 2026

The fifth and last tranche of second attempts on Stams records (collection
C-ugo5), after the four earlier revisit notes in `audits/`. With it every Stams
row left partial or unidentified by the first pass has had a second attempt.
The twenty-two are the remaining documents and scraps: letters, deeds, accounts
and registers, school exercises and small cut strips. No record is identified;
one moves from unidentified to partial and one rises from low to medium.

## Process

1. No new first readings were made: each record's preserved image-only
   reading stands.
2. Research by eleven sessions (Claude Fable 5.2), two records each, with the
   ledger row, the earlier report and review as baseline. Sessions kept their
   scripts with the evidence and were told that "cannot be read" is a valid
   result.
3. Six blind readers (Claude Fable 5.2) transcribed 125 renamed crops with no
   context, from a working directory that did not name the collection. The
   crops were screened for pencil notes and context words before reading. The
   blind readings confirmed, among others, the parallel letter formula of
   F-hy4i and F-t8sd, 'Argumen' on F-ulkj, 'iiij schöffel fesen' and 'altdorff'
   on F-5lit, the 1614 date on F-712u and 'Quarde Verwalt[..]' on F-zdro. They
   did not confirm 'Elß' (F-hui3), 'Bartl/Hartl' (F-a6zx), 'Függere' (F-zdro,
   now 'Fughere(?)'), the 1682 pencil year (F-kuvn) or several words in the
   low partials, which are now queried or dropped. On F-rnqk a blind reader
   gave 'posse' where the research and the coordinator read 'posco' (one long
   s, then c and o); 'posco' is kept and the disagreement recorded.
4. Three checkers reread the claims on the images with the blind readings in
   hand, and re-ran the outside sources (Lausser's urbar edition, Lindner's
   Stams profession list, Oberrauch and Schober for the Hall forest officers,
   the Hagenau attestations). They kept every status and corrected wording on
   twenty records.

## Unavailable sources

- manuscripta.at returned 403 to every request.
- The Tiroler Landesarchiv (Kammer copybooks, Hall Salzamt records) was not
  consulted.
- Several outside records (Oberrauch 1952, Schober 1989, the Lothen and
  Stegen family histories) were seen in search snippets only.

These are recorded as untested, not as negative results.
