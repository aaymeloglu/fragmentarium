# Stams fragments, second attempt on twenty-two archival documents, November 2026

A fourth tranche of second attempts on Stams records (collection C-ugo5),
after the three earlier revisit notes in `audits/`. All twenty-two are
archival documents: deeds and grants, letters, accounts, a court formulary and
Latin documents. No record changes status; several are reframed or gain an
outside anchor.

## Process

1. No new first readings were made: each record's preserved image-only
   reading stands.
2. Research by eleven sessions (Claude Fable 5.2), two records each, with the
   ledger row, the earlier report and review as baseline. monasterium.net was
   used only through a browser session.
3. Eight blind readers (Claude Fable 5.2) transcribed 137 renamed crops with no
   context. They could see the word "stams" in their working directory, so
   their readings of that name are not counted as independent; one crop
   (F-kqf1) showed a modern pencil date and was re-cut and re-read. The blind
   readings confirmed, among others, the St Bartholomew dating of F-c1cn, the
   struck sealer of F-zdff and the convent seal of F-0kh0; they did not
   confirm several reconstructions, which are now marked as such or dropped
   (F-tf3m's depot dispute, F-ham0's Partschins, F-nciq's dating clause,
   F-zdff's "Aicherin", F-4568's indiction).
4. Two checkers reread nine records on the images and outside records and
   corrected wording on all of them; the main corrections are named above.

## Unavailable sources

- manuscripta.at returned 403 to every request (host volumes Hs 23, Hs 56).
- Lebersorg's Stams chronicle (ed. Haidacher 2000) gave page-number hits only.
- The Tiroler Landesarchiv (Kammer copybooks, Hall Salzamt records) was not
  consulted.

These are recorded as untested, not as negative results.
