# Stams fragments, second attempt on twenty-two more open records, November 2026

A third tranche of second attempts on Stams records (collection C-ugo5),
after `audits/stams-revisit-2026-11-09.md` and
`audits/stams-revisit-2-2026-11-09.md`. The twenty-two were chosen from the 66
open Stams rows left: the remaining scraps of book hands, the polyphonic music
part leaves, the school exercises, and the letters, Latin documents and
bundles of papers with the most text. No record was identified; two move from
unidentified to partial, and several are reframed.

## Process

1. No new first readings were made: each record's preserved image-only
   reading stands.
2. Research by eleven sessions (Claude Fable 5.2), two records each, given the
   research brief, the earlier report and review, and the ledger row as the
   baseline. The brief now requires that a reading be called unprimed only if
   it stands verbatim in the preserved first reading.
3. Five blind readers (Claude Fable 5.2), given 89 renamed crops chosen by the
   research sessions and no context, transcribed the words that carried each
   result. They confirmed, among others, "Durch[l]: Camer" on F-j8cl,
   "[s]atisfa(ci)o(n)es" on F-4td2, "propst" on F-8u0s and "Pulsatum est:
   uisam si libet" on F-tema; they did not confirm the readings behind
   F-kd2f's John 15 suggestion, which was withdrawn.
4. Independent checkers reread F-4x8s and eleven partial records on the images
   and sources. They confirmed the Codex Iustinianus piece (about 25 words in
   order, read before opening the edition) and the Basel *Acta* placement, and
   corrected wording on F-4td2 ("possibly"), F-n1f4 (closest to Dietenberger,
   not his text), F-j8cl, F-wmq3, F-njn8 and F-otkb.

## Unavailable sources

- manuscripta.at returned 403 to every request.
- The Innsbruck Kammer copybooks (Tiroler Landesarchiv) were not consulted.
- Lebersorg's Stams chronicle (ed. Haidacher 2000) is available only as
  page-number hits, not text.
- CoMMA searches for several records did not run because the machine's search
  lock was held by other sessions.

These are recorded as untested, not as negative results.
