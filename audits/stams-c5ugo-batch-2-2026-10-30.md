# Stams uncatalogued fragments, batch 2, October 2026

The second batch from the 187 uncatalogued Stams fragments (collection
C-ugo5; title "x", shelfmark, material and dimensions only). Twenty
records were chosen from the triage of the collection, all rated likely
to be identifiable, across kinds: ten theological, literary or school
texts, two scientific or medical, four liturgical and four archival
documents.

## Process

1. Tile packets from native images; a modern pencilled slip (F-hhs4), a
   removal label (F-bjvu) and pencilled dates (F-t6yk) were masked.
   First readings by fresh model readers given only the prompt and tiles
   (`stams-2026-10-30b-image-only`), frozen before research. Packets of
   more than about 45 tiles lost images from the readers' context:
   F-hmwp was read by three readers by image range and the parts were
   joined; F-2ma4's first reader drafted text while its images had failed
   to load, so that response was discarded and a fresh reader read the
   packet again.
2. Research by ten sessions, two records each, with the research brief, or
   the document brief for the four deeds. Sessions that compared only OCR
   text or an online transcription were sent back to read the source's
   page images.
3. Checks. The coordinator reread one matched passage on the manuscript
   image against the source page image for F-hhs4, F-bwv7 and F-rdns, and
   the join between F-bjvu and F-qslo. For the other identifications a
   fresh reader chose a passage the researcher had not used, read it on the
   image before opening the source, and compared it on a page image. For
   the four deeds, a reader with no transcription was given neutral crops
   of the year words, dates, names, places and sums.

## Corrections made by the checks

- F-hhs4: the PL 76 page cited was one leaf off (cols. 127-128 for
  129-130).
- F-1m6r: a reported variant "Sic in duobus" was a misreading ("Sed et in
  duobus", as Friedberg); the gloss also covers VI 1.3.8.
- F-pfza: the verse range was off by two lines at each end; "ipsus
  gnatus" for "gnatam"; "school copy" dropped, since nothing on the leaf
  supports it.
- F-hmwp: agreement with the Leonine text against PL 64 is mixed, not
  consistent; one further piece placed (II.14); the r/v labels on piece 4
  are reversed.
- F-5z6w, F-x6cz, F-udt0: use statements reworded as inference. For
  F-5z6w the papal-court Ordinal was seen only in snippets; for F-udt0 the
  nocturn II psalm cues match neither Rein nor the 1584 print.
- F-1vn7: one responsory and four variants added; "office book", not
  breviary.
- F-s0lp: the deceased's name reads "[...]ristan", not "[Ch]ristof"; the
  date narrowing that rested on a Christof genealogy was dropped.
- F-t6yk: the fresh reader read "Tricesimo primo"; the coordinator reread
  "proximo venturo", which the calendar figures (dominical letter B,
  Septuagesima 13 February, Easter 17 April) confirm as 1530.
- F-mgy1: the first reading's "zwaintzigisten" (twentieth) is wrong; the
  year is [14]92. The fresh reader could not read the sums or the year's
  tens under holes; the coordinator read "ailffhundert" twice and
  "Zwa[y]vnd ne[w]nzigisten". The third Vöhlin's forename is uncertain
  (Erhard or Bernhard).

## Observations

- F-bjvu and F-qslo are consecutive bifolia of one twelfth- or
  thirteenth-century Lombard Psalter commentary: the text runs on across
  both joins.
- F-rdns and F-bwv7 may come from one *Legenda aurea* codex (same line
  pitch, column width and paragraph marks); not shown.
- F-rdns's Clement column stops mid-sentence with the rest of the ruled
  column blank.
- Several leaves were covers of the abbey's own registers: the
  Mittelkellner office's Urbare of 1577 (F-j6yr) and 1613 (F-2ma4), an
  Urbar of 1661-1666 (F-rdns), the register "15 Stambs 64" (F-s0lp).
