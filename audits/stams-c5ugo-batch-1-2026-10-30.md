# Stams uncatalogued fragments, batch 1, October 2026

On 30 October 2026 a Fragmentarium editor published 187 uncatalogued
fragments from Stams, Stiftsarchiv, in an unindexed collection (C-ugo5).
Each has the title "x" and only shelfmark, material and dimensions. A
triage of all 187 from images (kind, language, legibility, likely
promise) chose twelve records across kinds for this first batch.

## Process

1. First readings by five fresh model readers given only the packet prompt and
   image tiles (`stams-2026-10-30-image-only`). They had repository and search
   tools available and were told not to use them; each response states its
   exposures. Two are not blind in part: F-hgu4's overview shows a modern
   pencil note naming Enea Silvio and Regensburg, and F-ag0c's overviews show a
   typed transcription, whose spans the reader marked. The readings were
   committed before research began.
2. Research by six sessions, two records each, with the research brief, or the
   document brief for F-8spe and F-gtpi.
3. An independent check of every claim: a fresh reader reread one passage per
   record on the native image, where possible one the researcher had not
   read, compared it with the source on a page image where one exists, and
   tested the status against these conventions. All claims held. Corrections
   made in the reports: F-n2rx's Laurence chapter does contain the passage
   first reported missing (it differs in order and two short omissions);
   corrected loci or ranges for F-zq8i, F-27io and F-hgu4; F-2py3 claimed on
   two of its three texts; readings and variants on several records.

## Judgment calls for review

- **F-gtpi.** First read, researched and independently checked as
  "Dreiunddreissigisten" and dated [16]33 under Paul II, against a host
  register titled 1578. A Codex review of the PR read "Dreiundvierzigisten"
  on the image, confirmed by two fresh readers and by the coordinator: the
  letters after "vier" are "-tzig-", with no long s. The year is now [15]43
  under Paul I Käsinger, whose rule the Kaisheim local history gives as
  1540-1544 and Siebmacher as 1540-1542; the date rests on the first. The
  preserved readings keep the wrong year word; the report states the
  correction. The independent check had been asked to reread the dating
  line and confirmed the wrong reading, so a check by a reader who already
  holds the report's transcription in view is weak on single words.
- **Blind checks of the deeds.** Two fresh readers, given only crops and
  neutral questions, read the year, names and places of F-8spe and F-gtpi.
  They confirm F-8spe's "Funffundvierzigisten" (1545), "Bernhar[d]", Georg
  Engl[...] and the Pfleger and judge of Imst, and F-gtpi's Abbot Paulus,
  the Walthauserin, Johann[s], the son and daughter, Tartsch, and the
  endorsement's Johannes, Petronella and Wilhalm. Both read a forename
  Anthoni in F-gtpi line 3 where the reports had "anndern"; the report now
  records a possible further co-recipient, unresolved.
- **F-ag0c.** The researcher proposed partial; the checker proposed
  identified, scoped to *Audi tellus* on Frg. 7-11v and the added piece,
  where the Ubi strophes agree with AH on its page images. The expanded
  strophes naming Samson and Absalon are not read on any image (they come
  from the typed slip), and *Media vita* rests on an offset.
