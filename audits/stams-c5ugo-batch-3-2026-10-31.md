# Stams uncatalogued fragments, batch 3, October 2026

The third batch from the uncatalogued Stams fragments (collection C-ugo5;
title "x", shelfmark, material and dimensions only). Twenty records were
chosen from those the triage rated most likely to be identifiable: seven
theological or literary, two law or science, six liturgical and five
archival documents. Five other high-rated records were left for later: a
44-piece court record (F-lv0w), a printed map (F-mzu8) and three short or
generic liturgical pieces (F-l7tu, F-e0vk, F-wbkk).

## Process

1. Tile packets from native images. Modern additions that name or
   transcribe the text were kept from the readers: F-qqhf's typed
   transcription slip and pencilled dates and F-nbxz's typed slip were
   masked; F-fpb6's 1928 notes (canvases 2-5) were left out. F-m4fu and
   F-3h7h were read in part (two canvases each). First readings by fresh
   model readers given only the prompt and tiles
   (`stams-2026-10-31-image-only`), frozen before research. One reader
   reported that some images were removed from its context after it had
   read them; it reopened three and they matched its transcription.
2. Research by eleven sessions, one or two records each, with the
   research brief or the document brief. Sessions that compared only OCR,
   snippets or online transcriptions, or left a named candidate untested,
   were sent back.
3. Checks. For each literary and liturgical identification a fresh
   reader chose a passage the researcher had not used, read it on the
   image before opening the source, and compared it on a source page
   image. The coordinator read F-1tu1's Saturn passage against Strobl
   p. 237 on the page image. For the five documents, two fresh readers
   with no transcription were given 39 shuffled neutral crops of the
   names, dates, places and sums, and the coordinator reread every
   disagreement.

## Corrections made by the checks

- F-m4fu: leaves 2 and 3 are not consecutive (a leaf is lost after the
  Nativity of the Virgin); leaf 13 holds May saints and the Ascension, so
  it is not "the May part of the sanctorale"; a Hincmar lesson for
  Remigius was added.
- F-c7me: the printed waste follows the 1489 Basel edition page for page
  but is not the same setting; printer and edition left open. A marginal
  correction supplies the sixth and seventh reasons for Francis' name.
- F-nbxz: "procedi" was a misreading of "[f]aciendi"; the apostles' reply
  is abridged.
- F-gieu: the *Cogitis me* leaf continues §16; the lessons are
  continuous, not out of order. Two misreadings corrected.
- F-rvkg: Isa 60:12 omits "non"; 60:15 is a word-order change; "secular"
  and "central bifolium" hedged.
- F-3h7h: the inscription reads Benedict(en)pewren; Petronilla's prayers
  and most of Ulrich's, Afra's and Hilaria's Masses differ from the
  Missale Augustanum of 1491, so the Augsburg use is not established.
- F-gh8b: about one or two lines, not fourteen, are lost between pieces 2
  and 1; 15 September is probable only; the pieces are laid paper, as the
  catalogue says, not parchment. A Giles lesson was collated with the Vita
  S. Aegidii (BHL 93).
- F-1tu1: downgraded to partial; the fragment differs from the
  Donaueschingen manuscript in order and wording, but D could still lie
  behind the reworking.
- F-1ax4: the vicar is "de Piscatriis (?)"; the party is Giovannino de
  Barberiis (the fresh reader's "Joannis de barbeijs" was rejected on two
  rereads); the court was Spanish-imperial, not French as the triage had
  it.
- F-1qyf: "Velmair", not "Veltmair", in the clearer occurrence; the
  second witness reads "Eustachius Sta[..]".
- F-ns7l: the farm is Haunoldt; recorded as identified, since the action
  and parties are read and only the year and sum are lost.
- F-zdff: the abbot of Wilten is the requested sealer, which does not
  make Wilten the landlord; the wives' surnames are uncertain; a further name, Cristan
  Holtzna[..], was found; the triage's 1580 is not on the sheet, and the
  dorse year is unresolved.
- F-qqhf: downgraded to partial. "Ewirt[...]" is the formula for a
  married couple, not a buyer's name, and the buyer's wife's name came
  only from the pencilled transcription. The fresh reader could read
  almost nothing on the faded crops; the names rest on the research
  reading and agree with the archivist's typed slip.

## Observations

- F-c7me is probably from the same *Legenda aurea* codex as F-n2rx
  (batch 1): same line pitch, text block, script and sewing stations.
  F-nbxz is a different, two-column copy.
- Several pieces were covers or linings of the abbey's own volumes:
  F-ns7l of a Kelleramt Urbar "Ao 92" (binding date unknown), F-zdff of Hs. 125, F-nbxz of a
  volume bound in 1661-1666.
- Two documents are from outside Tyrol: F-1ax4 from Brescia (1514) and
  F-1qyf from Ingolstadt (1560s).

## Not done

- The other 135 records of the collection.
- F-htlf (Berlin, Ms. germ. oct. 449, f. 84r, not digitised), F-0w8s
  (Bernard of Parma's Casus, Hostiensis and Henricus de Merseburg untested), F-1tu1 (Richter 1969
  on the Berthold tradition not reached), F-joy1 (the sermon itself).
- Coens' edition of the Vita S. Magni (Analecta Bollandiana 81) was seen
  only in snippets.
- No outside record of any of the five documents was found; monasterium,
  the Tiroler Landesarchiv, the Brescia and Bergamo state archives and the
  Bavarian archives for Frauenchiemsee were not searched page by page.
- No palaeographer has reviewed the readings.

## Review

An independent Codex review of the PR (31 October 2026) agreed with the
identifications and found four claims that went beyond the evidence, all
corrected: F-ns7l's "before 1592" (the register label does not date the
deed), F-zdff's Wilten lordship (a sealer is not thereby the landlord),
F-0w8s's exclusion of Bernard of Parma's Casus (tested only against the
decretal's incipit) and F-1tu1's exclusion of the Donaueschingen manuscript
as a source of the reworking.
