# Stams uncatalogued fragments, batch 9, November 2026

The ninth and last batch from the uncatalogued Stams fragments (collection
C-ugo5; title "x", shelfmark, material and dimensions only). The 25 records
left after batch 8: F-lv0w (44 canvases) and F-tnqw (strips carrying modern
typed transcriptions), skipped in earlier batches, and 23 "little text"
records. Every record of the collection has now had at least one attempt.

## Process

1. Contact sheets and a layout review: masks on 13 records and on F-lv0w (host-volume notes
   with years, typed transcription slips on F-tnqw and F-7yec, modern sheets
   between F-lv0w's strips), rotations on F-tnqw and F-q7x0, tighter areas.
   F-lv0w was read in four packets of ten to twelve canvases; its first
   reading is the four readings `stams9-2026-11-01-image-only` and
   `stams9-2026-11-01-lv0w-{a2,b1,b2}-image-only`. The layout review missed a
   typed transcription slip on F-gtxi; its first reader saw it and stopped,
   that reading was set aside unfrozen, the slip was masked, and a fresh
   reader made the reading that is preserved. F-i1x7's reader was sent back
   to read sixteen tiles it had skipped as too faint.
2. Research by thirteen sessions, one to three records each, with the
   document brief for the archival records.
3. One batched CoMMA search after research (Latin phrases of eight records,
   F-stvt as a control): the control surfaced civil-law manuscripts; the
   F-14ex hits are Huguccio manuscripts; no new lead.
4. Checks. Independent checkers read untranscribed passages of F-stvt, F-sqjv,
   F-tnqw, F-q7x0, F-h4ki and F-lv0w (leaves 1-4) on the image before the
   source and collated them with page images. The coordinator read a matched
   passage of F-stvt, F-sqjv and F-tnqw on the image. For the eight records
   with archival text, two fresh readers with no transcription read 50
   shuffled neutral crops of dating clauses and names in two pools, and an
   adjudicator per pool read every crop on the image before the research
   reading.

## Corrections made by the checks

- F-stvt: strips 2 and 3, which research left unplaced, are also Blanot
  (fol. 277v and 278r of the 1568 print), placed by runs of matching words on
  stained surfaces; three first-reading words were misreadings.
- F-q7x0 and F-h4ki: lowered from identified to partial; the named texts
  cover only part of each record. F-q7x0 has four corner pieces, not seven,
  and five readings were corrected.
- F-tnqw: the archivist's slips already cite the MGH edition and the loci, so
  the identification is recorded as known; 168-2r carries a later addition.
- F-lv0w: Grumpach is a canon in the Würzburg diocese, not of the cathedral;
  "Leodiensis" is legible; the June 1434 day comes from a modern slip.
- F-gtxi: the chaplain's surname, Freundsberg, St. Petersberg, the Baurecht
  and the 1407-1586 bound rest on the modern typed slip, not on the leaf.
- F-i1x7: the century, feast and 15 December 1533 come from a pencil note;
  "Lienhart" and "Jorg" are not on the image.
- F-7yec: the 1507/1508 year word is unreadable; the day and 1508 come from a
  pencil note.
- F-g1bu: the word read as "Weing[arten]" is uncertain, so the issuing house
  (Stams or Wilten) is open.
- F-a6zx: "Hanns Hayim" is "ganz heÿm gesetzt"; F-uahm: "Kauffschilling" is
  not supported; F-sqjv: an "m" on the right scrap is modern pencil.
