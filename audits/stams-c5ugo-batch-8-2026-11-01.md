# Stams uncatalogued fragments, batch 8, November 2026

The eighth batch from the uncatalogued Stams fragments (collection C-ugo5;
title "x", shelfmark, material and dimensions only). Twenty-five low-promise
records: the sixteen the triage found to carry some legible text, and the nine
"little text" records with the most text or the most distinctive layout. F-lv0w
and F-tnqw are again left for later. Most are early-modern German papers:
letters and drafts, accounts, deeds and school exercises.

## Process

1. Contact sheets and a layout review: 23 mask boxes on 12 records (pencil
   "aus Urbar"/"aus Hs" host notes with years, a 1965 provenance note),
   F-2yl6 rotated, missed strips added. Tile packets from native images, one
   packet per record, at most 45 images; first readings by fresh model readers
   given only the prompt and tiles (`stams8-2026-11-01-image-only`). No reader
   stopped on the image limit. On F-zd3n the second face's right page had no
   tiles, and its reader read it from enlarged overview crops and said so.
   Two readers wrote local crop paths into their responses; those paths were
   replaced with "[reader scratch]" before the readings were committed.
2. Research by fourteen sessions, one or two records each, with the document
   brief for the archival records.
3. One batched CoMMA search after research, Latin phrases of seven records:
   no lead.
4. Checks. Independent checkers read untranscribed passages of F-bm2h, F-d71j,
   F-ibi1, F-y3wr and F-n1f4 on the image before the source and collated them
   with page images where reachable, and checked the book that dates F-2r2o
   against VD17. The coordinator read a matched passage of F-bm2h, F-d71j and
   F-ibi1 on the image. For the seventeen records with archival text, two
   fresh readers with no transcription read 97 shuffled neutral crops of
   dating clauses and names in three pools, and an adjudicator per pool read
   every crop on the image before the research reading.

## Corrections made by the checks

- F-njn8: 82-12 is dated 21 July 1565 ("ainundzwainzigisten"), not 29 July;
  the Bohemian regnal year is the 17th. 82-2 is M.D.xxxv, 2 March 1535, not
  1540. The 82-4 docket year is [15]59; the 82-9 place is uncertain.
- F-xj55: the year is 1635 ("Funffunddreissig"), not 1631, and the line read
  as a monastery is the day, "dem lesten Junij".
- F-zdro: the country word is "Hungerlan[d](?)" rather than "Engenland", so the
  research's 1588 English-armada dating was dropped.
- F-ibi1: the six lines above the letter are the end of the expanded
  Ps.-Gregory *Statuimus*, printed from Stams Cod. 17 (snippets only), and four
  readings there were corrected; the letter is the long form found in Vat. lat.
  1361, not the short form edited from Leipzig 276.
- F-bm2h: the red headings are the standard table headings, not paraphrases of
  the Summa's prologues; the q.37 heading was reread.
- F-d71j: col. 3 of 104_2 is not in Peltier; the Holtnicker attribution is
  supported by Klosterneuburg CCl 451's rubrics (catalogue only).
- F-y3wr: the variants favour the Frankfurt printings of 1561-1587 over the
  Cologne 1620 edition the research compared.
- F-2r2o: the dating book is the third part of Neuburger's *Conciones
  rurales*, first printed 1668; "after 1659" was too early.
- F-n1f4: the Proverbs text is an abridged excerpt, not a paraphrase; 1664 is
  the date inside the copied model letter.
- F-zd3n: a modern pencil note reads "1534 2[6/8] Juni" (not "20 Jan."), a few
  weeks before the 1534 grant used for context, so no date is asserted.
- F-712u: the administrator's surname on the leaf is not "Unterrainer";
  that name remains an outside lead only.
- F-ham0: the day is 17 (15 possible), not 27, and the endorsement does not
  support "Zehent" (tithe).
- F-j8cl: the second addressee is "Den Tirolischen C[..]", chancellor or
  chamber; the month favours September.
- F-2zug: the year's third digit reads as 3, so 1639, with 1609 an
  alternative.
- F-tf3m, F-pnzn, F-yxob, F-1gv6, F-vzoy, F-hy4i: names and words queried or
  corrected (e.g. "Kirchleuth", "Klainhanser", "[M]ichael(?)",
  "Herge(t/d)ingers(?)").

## Review

An independent review of the pull request agreed with the three identifications and with F-njn8's 21 July 1565, and raised three points on F-zd3n, all accepted: the date is "not established" rather than "lost", since the unread block on fol. 2v may hold it; the report's confidence now agrees with the ledger's high, with the basis stated (action and all parties read, one element not established); and the ledger's link now points at the Fragmentarium record, since the 1534 grant is a related act, not a record of the judgment.
