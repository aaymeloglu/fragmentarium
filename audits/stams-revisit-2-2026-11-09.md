# Stams fragments, second attempt on twenty more open records, November 2026

A second attempt on twenty Stams records (collection C-ugo5) that the first
pass of October-November 2026 left partial or unidentified, following the
twelve in `audits/stams-revisit-2026-11-09.md`. The twenty were chosen from
the 86 open Stams rows left after that round as those with literary,
liturgical, scholastic or school text, where a printed or edited source could
exist; archival documents, music and the most fragmentary scraps were left
for later.

## Process

1. No new first readings were made: each record's preserved image-only
   reading stands, and the new source-assisted readings are saved under the
   same reading names.
2. Research by ten sessions (Claude Fable 5.2), two records each, given the
   research brief, the earlier report and any later corrections or reviews,
   with the ledger row as the baseline. They were asked to state negatives as
   searches and to carry qualifiers through every field.
3. Independent checkers (Claude Fable 5.2) reread each identification on the
   images before opening the source and tested alternatives, and checked the
   nine partial records that gained a new source. They corrected wording on
   all three identifications and on seven partials. The largest corrections:
   F-mnxg's earlier reading as a commentary on Cicero, *De senectute* 16.56 was
   not reproduced by two readers who had no candidate text and is withdrawn;
   F-pmci's set-off, first described as a Gothic hand of an undetermined
   medical work, is the offset of F-53hg; F-h4ki's words "egrot[um]",
   "tetigit linguam" and "organum meum in" were read only with the source in
   view and are no longer used as evidence.
4. The coordinator read one matched passage of each identification on the
   manuscript image and on the source page. A separate reader, given eight
   renamed crops from the three identified records and no source or report,
   transcribed them. It agreed with F-da74 in all three crops ("hoc totu(m) ad
   salute(m) mea(m)", "sine hospit(i)o ... in asina nudu(m) pendentem i(n)
   patib(u)lo", "aust(r)o calido ... lac(ri)maru(m)") and with F-g51l in the
   July names. On F-h4ki it read the rubric "Omelia . v . be[...]" and "-s
   ille", but none of the three words named above.

## Unavailable sources

- manuscripta.at returned 403 to every request.
- The Kiel manuscript viewer did not answer scripts; its pages were fetched
  through a browser.
- e-codices could not be reached (St. Gallen and Zürich copies of the
  *Iatromathematisches Hausbuch* calendar).
- CoMMA searches for several records did not run because the machine's single
  search lock was held by other sessions.

These are recorded as untested, not as negative results.
