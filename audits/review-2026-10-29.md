## What the failures have in common

- Two records (F-xukl, F-1kdv) had the right text and the wrong physical
  account, because the catalogue's division into pieces was taken as given.
  F-1kdv's report already noted an unexplained blank in its strip [2]
  placement; that was the signal.
- One (F-1kdv) mixed gloss and main text in the reading.
- One (F-v7a4) identified an object in the images other than the one the
  record catalogues; its report said so and still credited the record.
- One (F-kcu7) accepted structural agreement in a genre where commentaries
  share structure by design.

METHOD.md and the research brief now cover each of these: the result must be
about the catalogued object; the catalogue's division into pieces is tested
before pieces are placed apart; gloss and main text are separated before
reading; school-tradition commentaries need verbatim agreement on more than
one face or piece to be identified.
