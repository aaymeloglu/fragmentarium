# Stams uncatalogued fragments, batch 4, October 2026

The fourth batch from the uncatalogued Stams fragments (collection C-ugo5;
title "x", shelfmark, material and dimensions only). Twenty records: four
left over from the triage's highest-rated group (F-mzu8, F-l7tu, F-e0vk,
F-wbkk) and sixteen from its middle group, chosen for legible text in an
identifiable genre: nine theological, literary or school texts, five
liturgical, two law, one computus, one printed map and two archival
documents. The 44-piece court record F-lv0w was again left for later.

## Process

1. Tile packets from native images; F-pk0m's modern blue note (naming the
   printed host book) was masked. F-cb2e and F-31t6 were read in part. First
   readings by fifteen fresh model readers given only the prompt and tiles
   (`stams4-2026-10-31-image-only`), frozen before research. Readers reported
   only modern shelfmarks and binding notes on the leaves; two said they
   recognised liturgical or psalm text while reading and flagged where that
   might have biased a letter.
2. Research by twelve sessions, one or two records each. Seven were sent back
   (one twice): to compare on page images where they had only snippets or an
   electronic text (F-qcam, F-o7j1), to test a named candidate (Cistercian
   missals for F-l7tu, F-v82x, F-e0vk and F-wbkk; German sermon collections
   for F-4hud; writing-master books and hymnals for F-w7wh; BnF NAL 999 for
   F-vjii), or to read unread parts (F-31t6 hand 2).
3. Checks. For every identification a fresh checker chose a passage the
   research had not used, read it on the image before opening the source,
   and compared it on a source page image. For the two documents, two fresh
   readers with no transcription were given 21 shuffled neutral crops, and
   the coordinator reread every disagreement on the image.

## Corrections made by the checks

- F-qcam: the grid is an Easter table (each cell letter gives the Easter
  date; about 90% of some 110 legible cells agree with computed Julian
  Easter), not Peter of Dacia's new-moon calendar; the research's cycle of
  1361-1436 and alternative year 1368 were withdrawn.
- F-py5e: the abbot's name, read tentatively as Simon, is not
  legible and was left unread; "Ehrenreich" (from an outside record) is not
  on the sheet; "zum Steg", not "beim Steg"; "Fried[berg]" not read.
- F-31t6: Rampegolus is claimed only for the two items collated (81_9 and
  81_10); 81_8 and 81_11 were not found in the print.
- F-l7tu, F-v82x: a "Meritis et intercessionibus" blessing and "custodiat me"
  also occur in Salzburg-province diocesan books, so Cistercian use was
  lowered to "probably", on the narrower features that remain diagnostic.
  Carthusian use is untested.
- F-w7wh: the verse is Nikolaus Herman's rhyming of Mathesius, not
  Mathesius' own; two of three "unmatched" readings were misreadings of
  verses in the print.
- F-o7j1: Johannes Andreae's *Novella* was tested and excluded for face A;
  "hodie" is present after all.
- F-vjii: a sentence absent from NAL 999 shows the Stams copy is not the same
  text form; Alan of Lille's authorship is Schneyer's listing, seen in
  snippets only.
- F-xnhr: the fragment's own rubric names "...de Aquilegia"; a face the
  research left out (35_3v) and the reading order were added.
- F-e0vk: a red folio number cxvij fixes which face was the recto; Eph 4:7-13
  is absent on direct reading, not by line counting.
- F-wbkk: the two-column slip layout was dropped; the responsory allocation
  claim was withdrawn.
- F-cb2e, F-pk0m, F-p8ik, F-mzu8, F-3wa4, F-o5pg: contents, readings,
  page-image references and lost-line counts corrected; F-mzu8's 1629 is the
  edition's date, not a date on the pieces.

## Observations

- F-o5pg and F-vjii, which the catalogue joins in reconstruction F-dr4l
  (not public), agree in scale, line pitch, column width, hand and rubric
  style; one codex is plausible, not proven.
- Several pieces were covers or linings of later books and records: F-w7wh of
  Urbare labelled 1594 and 1595, F-mzu8 probably of Hs 287 or 308 (pencil notes), F-py5e of a
  charter of 1572 until 1963, F-pk0m, per a modern note, of an
  *Officia propria Brixinensia* printed at Brixen in 1673 (host copy not checked).
- Not all the waste is medieval: a 1629 printed map, a late sixteenth-century
  calligraphic verse sheet (F-w7wh) and a deed of the mid-sixteenth century (after 1546, probably before 1558).

## Not done

- The other 115 records of the collection.
- F-4hud's sermon collection (Melk and Klosterneuburg manuscripts on
  manuscripta.at were not reachable); F-w7wh's Psalm 128 paraphrase;
  F-vjii's Marian sermon; F-31t6 items 81_4-8 and 81_11; F-mzu8's verso
  offset.
- The use of the missal and breviary leaves: no medieval Brixen, Salzburg,
  Freising, Benedictine or Carthusian missal was compared on page images.
- No outside record of either document was found; the Tiroler Landesarchiv
  fief records and Tarneller's *Hofnamen des Burggrafenamtes* were not
  consulted.
- No palaeographer has reviewed the readings.

## Review

An independent Codex review of the PR (31 October 2026) agreed with the
identifications and found three claims that went beyond the evidence, all
corrected: F-qcam's table years were taken as the date of the copy (tables
were drawn up ahead and copied later unchanged); F-cb2e was called an
abridgement, when the comparison shows only that it is shorter than
Clm 3764 and the direction of change is open; and F-l7tu and F-v82x were
said not to be copies of Fribourg L 305 on the strength of small variants,
which cannot exclude dependence.

A later pass against the report brief's checklist of overreaching claims
(1 November 2026) qualified further statements: Augsburg use is excluded only
for the printed order of 1510/1555; the Cistercian features of F-l7tu and
F-v82x are not shown to be exclusive; F-vjii's dependence on NAL 999 and
F-xnhr's relation to the Paris copy are left open (and "school version"
dropped); F-py5e's wards are "children", as the text says, and its window
admits 1562-1564; F-c1cn's issuer is "probably" the abbot; F-31t6's Kiel
attribution is doubtful rather than impossible; F-3wa4's other commentaries
are untested rather than set aside; and smaller wording in F-e0vk, F-wbkk,
F-w7wh, F-qcam and F-4hud.
