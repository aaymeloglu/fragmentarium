# CoMMA search of the unresolved records, 20 October 2026

Every partial and unidentified record in the ledger was searched against the
full CoMMA corpus with `tools/comma.py`: 193 records, of which 127 had enough
secure text to query (103 partial, 24 unidentified). This is the first search
of these records in manuscript text rather than printed editions. The blind
controls for the same search are in [comma-controls-2026-10-20.md](comma-controls-2026-10-20.md).

## Queries

For each record, a helper session that did no searching turned the preserved
readings into ordered passages, expanded and broken at every gap. It used the
image-only and source-assisted readings, the report, and Latin quoted in the
ledger note. The basis of each passage is recorded in
[`comma-2026-10-20/queries.json`](comma-2026-10-20/queries.json). Records marked
unusable had too little secure text, had music only or no text, or had their
legible text filed under another record. All queries ran in one pass, each
alongside a shuffled copy of its own words.

## Screening

23 records had a top hit with a margin of 2 or more (partial), or 1.5 or more
(unidentified). For each one, a separate session read the CoMMA text around the
hit, collated it with the fragment's readings and checked what the manuscript
contains. At this stage it saw machine text only.

| Outcome | Records |

| The unresolved text found | F-h8oz, F-rmhf, F-8zoy (a source), F-67mf (two strips the record did not cover) |
| A further copy of text the ledger already identifies | F-yjo1, F-2fz6, F-b8xt, F-k81e, F-wbbg, F-pleo; base texts of a commentary: F-dr6y, F-8wp3 (*Doctrinale*), F-mfvu (Priscian) |

| Chance | F-4r0j, F-koak |

None of the 24 unidentified records produced a lead.

## Verification

The leads were then checked on page images of the fragment and of the
witnesses, and the witnesses' attributions in catalogues, repertories and
prints. The full evidence is in each fragment's report or ledger note.

- **F-h8oz**, identified: Bernardus Compostellanus junior, *Lectura* on Decretals
  I, on Innocent IV's *Statuimus*. Arras 590 and Reims 708 were checked on their
  images, and the Venice 1588 print on its page images. The print lacks the
  auditor case the fragment shares with both manuscripts; this was checked on
  the fol. 204v image. That gap is why Durantis was the nearest printed
  parallel. [Report](../fragments/F-h8oz/README.md).
- **F-rmhf**, identified: Peter of Reims, Sunday sermons on the epistles and
  gospels, Schneyer IV nos. 85 and 93-94. Troyes 303 and Amiens 268 were
  checked on their images, across four passages, two of them not used in
  discovery. [Report](../fragments/F-rmhf/README.md).
- **F-8zoy**, partial: Philip the Chancellor's Psalter sermons on Ps 19 are the
  source of the Passion-by-the-hours section, checked on BnF lat. 14594 and
  Soissons 65. The compilation itself remains unknown.
- **F-67mf**, partial: the lower strip is from a noted breviary for two Sundays
  after Pentecost, with Jerome's lessons on Mt 18 (checked against BnF lat.
  17991). The IIv homily is still unscanned.
- Ledger notes were also extended where a hit settled a smaller question:
  - F-jaob's five reasons for scourging are Peter Lombard's own text (Sent. IV
    d. 15 c. 2), checked against the Quaracchi page. Its report is corrected.
  - The *Catholicon* is ruled out for F-8srm.
  - Reims 683 is a further copy of F-wbbg's reworked tonsure passage.

All comparisons were made by research sessions (Claude). There has been no
human review.
