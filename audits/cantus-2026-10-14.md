# Chant records rechecked with Cantus — 14 October 2026

`tools/cantus.py` adds Cantus Index (standard chant texts, Cantus IDs, concordances across the
network) and the Cantus Database (manuscript readings) to the sources. Four ledger records with
chant were rechecked with it. None reached an identification; the gains are negatives across
Cantus that earlier runs could not record, and one ledger correction.

| Fragment | Result | What Cantus added |
|---|---|---|
| F-10zj | partial, low (unchanged) | Hymn 1, Martyris Christi (008347), is in 25 sources across central Europe and Italy, mostly at Vespers, so it does not place the book. No Lawrence hymn in Cantus, including the Matins hymns of the sources that have 008347, has the strophes of hymn 2. |
| F-wbbg | partial, medium (unchanged) | No verse of the neumed Easter dialogue is in Cantus or among the Visitatio and play items there; Lipphardt III-VI and Young were searched in Google Books. Lipphardt I-II remain unsearched. |
| F-333n | partial, medium (unchanged) | The neumed cover and the Juvencus sheets in these images are the separate records F-dd8g and F-q01a; the ledger now says so. The cover's chants for Lent IV and Passion Sunday were placed with Cantus IDs; their order is the common Roman one. |
| F-drrc | not identified (unchanged) | The only Cantus text with "carni s-" (Beata Agnes, Vienna Cod. 1890: "carni spurcitias") is excluded: the exposed letter after long s has no descender. |

Liturgical texts recur across books and uses, so a chant usually gives the feast and service, not
a manuscript. METHOD now says so under "Liturgical books". Readings in this run were assisted
(the earlier readings were known); the coordinator checked the F-333n chant reading and the F-drrc
letters on the images.
