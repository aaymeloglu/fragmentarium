# Editor review of Stams and Antwerp identifications, November 2026

A Fragmentarium editor looked at the first eight or nine records from the
batches of 30 and 31 October 2026 and reported on 1 November 2026. The
identifications he checked held. Changes made:

| Record | Finding | Change |
|---|---|---|
| F-ln58 | The attribution to Nicolaus de Byard is correct; Schneyer over-ascribes sermons, so his second listing under Robert of Sorbon carries no weight. | "(attributed)" dropped from the title and identification; the Sorbon listing described as an over-ascription. Status unchanged. |
| F-1ax4 | The vicar is Hieronymus de Pischariis, not Piscatriis. | Checked on the native image (canvas 1, region 1450,2130,700,110): the letter after "c" is an h with a looped ascender, and the r carries an abbreviation stroke, "pisch(a)rijs". The research reader and the fresh reader had both taken the h for a t; the image-only first reading had "pischerijs". Regest, transcription and ledger corrected; the surname dropped from "not claimed". |
| F-c7me | The printed sheets are Kesler's 1492 edition; the bifolium is from the same manuscript as F-n2rx. | The 1492 edition (Basel: Kesler, 19 Feb. 1492; GW M32486) compared on the Lisbon copy (BNP inc. 56, image 18): the distinction 1 piece agrees line for line, including "significātes:cuiusmodi" and "cuꝫ", the readings that ruled out 1489. Edition and same-manuscript claims moved out of "not claimed". F-n2rx now points to F-c7me. |

The editor also noted that secular and monastic use in lectionaries and
breviaries is often left uncertain; most human cataloguers cannot tell them
apart either. That is the existing practice (use stays open unless the
evidence decides it, as for F-5z6w), so no rule changed.

Frozen readings were not edited.
