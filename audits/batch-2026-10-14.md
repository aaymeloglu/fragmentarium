# Twenty-three-fragment batch of 14 October 2026

Twenty records were chosen from the 14 October catalogue sweep
(`data/unidentified-2026-10-14.csv`), and three more were added when the tile
step showed several of the twenty to carry almost no visible text. This is a
selected batch, not a random sample, and its yield is not a success rate for
the method.

## Selection

`tools/survey.py` listed the sweep's records without ledger rows, with native
image sizes, and made contact sheets. The forty records left from the earlier
sweep (non-starters and records passed over in the batches of 13 October) were
not reconsidered. The catalogue text of forty candidates was fetched with
`tools/catalogue.py` and read, including linked descriptions. The twenty
chosen were prose texts at roughly 25 to 200 pixels per millimetre whose
catalogue does not already name the text. Records with musical notation and
little text, offsets, and titles that are already identifications were not
chosen.

When the tiles were cut, five of the twenty proved nearly blank or very faint
on the published images (F-42s6, F-q4f8, F-s4q7, F-2mwf, F-67w1). They were
kept and recorded, and three reserves were added (F-owwe, F-cl1d, F-0qkl);
those also proved to carry little or no legible fragment text.

## First readings

A helper session that saw the catalogue text and reduced overviews, and did
not read or search the text, chose the tile areas (`tools/tilepacket.py`).
Seven fresh reader sessions, given only their packets and the reader brief,
wrote one response per packet. All twenty-three readings were frozen and
committed before any source search.

Limits:

- The coordinator had seen reduced images of every record before the readings
  were frozen.
- Several readers listed their packet directories to learn the image names,
  or made enlarged and contrast-enhanced crops of their tiles; each says so in
  its response.
- One reader reported that parts of two tiles and the overviews of F-kaqh did
  not reach it, so the first reading of faces 1 and 2 is thin; the research
  session read those faces itself.
- For F-yybk the packet had one tile, because the cache held only ten of the
  record's thirteen images; the research session fetched the joint image
  (VDSFalz) at native size.
- One packet (F-36nn) held 26 tiles.

## Source research

Eleven research sessions took one to five records each with
`prompts/research-brief.md`. For about half the records the session found no
connected phrase to search; those results are not negatives, and most of them
need UV or multispectral images.

Six sessions were sent back once, for twenty to twenty-five minutes, with
named candidates to read:

- F-mgx2: the first pass located one tab in the Glossa ordinaria on
  Lamentations 1:16, a sentence Martin of León also copies. The second pass
  located the other tab in the Glossa on 1:17. Full-text searches of Martin
  did not find this passage; that weakens him as an alternative but does not
  conclusively exclude him.
- F-36nn: Peraldus, John of San Gimignano's Summa de exemplis II as the
  containing work, and the Fasciculus morum were read and excluded.
- F-fpo3: the Oculus sacerdotis (Penn Ms. Codex 721) and the Pupilla oculi
  (Paris 1510) were read; both pair the Carta de foresta with canon-law case
  lists, but neither list holds the fragment's entries.
- F-63r4: Peraldus's De avaritia and Bromyard's Summa praedicantium were
  excluded by full-text search; John of Wales's Communiloquium only on poor
  OCR.
- F-2nxm: Jacobus de Voragine's Sermones de sanctis, the Legenda aurea and
  Jacques de Lausanne were excluded on OCR text.
- F-kaqh: Hugo de Prato Florido's sermons were excluded; Schneyer's repertory
  was scanned for sermons on the same Lenten days.

## Coordinator's checks

For F-yybk the coordinator read lines 8-11 of the curled corner (canvas 0)
on an enlargement against Google Books snippets of Otto's edition, p. 561; no
page image of the edition was reachable. For F-mgx2 the coordinator read tab A
lines 1-2 against the Lyon 1545 Glossa page image (MDZ bsb10141269, scan 393).

## What the results are

- F-yybk: Johannes Dacus, sophisma De gradibus formarum (ed. Otto 1955,
  pp. 554-555 and 561). The catalogue title was a genre guess. The source text
  rests on edition snippets, which the report states.
- F-mgx2: Glossa ordinaria on Lamentations 1:16-17, two short tabs.
- Partial: F-36nn (a moral compilation, not a medical treatise; two of its
  sources), F-fpo3 (a canon-law case list from an English pastoral manual of
  the Oculus kind), F-kaqh (Lenten weekday sermons, not a treatise), F-x33a
  (quotes the Rule of Benedict), F-r1e2 (a question on complexio), F-k0gn (a
  probable antiphon).
- Catalogue corrections without an identification: F-2nxm is not a psalter
  commentary; F-42s6 is not from the manuscript of F-2nxm; F-3u3b is the same
  object as part of F-ho7z; F-0qkl's legible table indexes the host volume.

## Review corrections, 14 October 2026

Codex retained both published identifications after inspecting the Vienna corner
and both Gubbio tabs. Comparison used saved Otto snippets for Vienna and the
Lyon 1545 page images for Gubbio. The Vienna joint locus was not independently
verified in this review, and partial results were assessed only at ledger level.

- F-yybk line 8 appears to read `q(ui)a(?) ens q(uo)d e(st) s(ub)[...]`,
  consistent with the catalogue, rather than the initial assisted `p(ro)pt(er)`.
  The report now preserves this apparent variant and its unresolved continuation.
  The comparison table covers ten corner lines (2-11); line 1 is illegible.
  The bracketed `non` remains recorded without asserting what Otto's brackets
  mean, since his editorial conventions were unavailable.
- F-mgx2: negative searches weaken the Martin of León alternative without
  conclusively excluding it. Ragged surviving line ends are compatible with
  gloss layout but do not establish the original page layout.

The original readings are preserved unchanged. The line 8 correction is saved in
`fragments/F-yybk/readings/review-2026-10-14-source-assisted.json`.
