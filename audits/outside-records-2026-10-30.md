# Outside records for the Stams documents, October 2026

earlier reported as unreachable: the Regesta Imperii open-data export
(`tools/ri.py`), monasterium.net and the Austrian State Archives catalogue
(both in a browser). No outside record conflicts with a reading on a sheet.
