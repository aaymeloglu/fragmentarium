Status: IDENTIFIED. Bernardus Compostellanus iunior (d. c. 1267), Lectura in primum librum Decretalium, on X 1.6.23 Bonae memoriae (end of the gloss Duximus confirmandam) and X 1.6.24 Querelam (glosses Querelam, De novo, Petebat, Subtractam), i.e. the title De electione et electi potestate. The text is canonistic, not Roman law: the "ff." citations are the commentator's allegations of the Digest.

The two columns are consecutive: between the foot of column A ("quod ultimo et immediate") and the head of column B ("Secus autem est") the print has c. 8 lines ("Ad hoc dicitur quod clerici actores ... quando eram in possessione interessendi electioni sive colligendi"), which fits the lines lost by trimming at the foot of the leaf.

Notes and queries

  • The leaf's lemma at A1 is read as Duximus confirmandam, matching the print's lemma "Duximus confirmandam.]". Friedberg's X 1.6.23 ends "electionem memorati archiepiscopi confirmantes"; the lemma reflects the Vulgate wording, not an error.
  • A20 "ut s. ostendi" (= ut supra ostendi), not "ut Host.". No Hostiensis citation was found, so the text is consistent with a 1260s author.
  • Marginal red bracket lines and pilcrows correspond to the section breaks in the print ("¶ Sed quia", "¶ De novo", "¶ Dicitur autem", "¶ Item si capitulum").
  • The blue initial at B41 is read as P (Petebat, the narratio of X 1.6.24); the print gives the equivalent gloss under the lemma Subtractum. The lemma differs, the gloss text is the same.
  • Bernardus Compostellanus iunior's Lectura covers book 1 only and survives in few manuscripts; a 14th-century copy in a Flemish binding is a notable witness. Catalogue title "Roman law" should be corrected to canon law (Decretals commentary).

Ruled out

  • Corpus iuris civilis text and Accursian gloss: the "ff." references are allegations inside a canonistic argument (postulatio, legatus, papa, capitulum, electio), not Digest text.
  • Decretals Glossa ordinaria (Bernardus Parmensis), Innocent IV, Hostiensis, Johannes Andreae Novella, Antonius de Butrio, Panormitanus: the phrase search hit these only for the topic (possessio iuris eligendi); none has the wording "pone quod unum annum vel plures semel vel bis omisi petere pensionem", which the Compostellanus print does.
  • Corpus Corporum and Corpus Thomisticum: not applicable (canonistic lectura, not PL/Aquinas).
