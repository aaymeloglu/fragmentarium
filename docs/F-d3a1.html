Readings and comparison details

F-d3a1: Konrad Vos (or Vor), Kellner of Tirol for Duke Sigmund, grants Hermann Aichhorn called Francken a house and garden at Meran zu Erbrecht und Baurecht, 22 July 1454

This parchment sheet in the archive of the Cistercian abbey of Stams is the original of a tenancy grant. Konrad Vos or Vor, Kellner and Amtmann at Tirol for Duke Sigmund of Austria, acting in the duke's name and with full power, grants Hermann Aichhorn, called Francken, and his heirs a house with a garden at Meran zu Erbrecht und Baurecht, a hereditary building tenure. They may sell only to people acceptable to the duke as tenants, must keep the buildings in repair, and pay a yearly rent to the duke's Kellneramt at Tirol on St Martin's day according to the Kellneramt's Urbar books. The Kellner promises warranty and announces his own pendant seal. The sheet was later used as a wrapper in the binding of a Stams incunable.

The Fragmentarium record is Stams, Stiftsarchiv, Frg. 199, catalogued as "Charter / Legacy" (Erbschaftsurkunde), German, 22 July 1454, 395 x 237 mm, with no host volume.

  • Identification: identified, high. The regest is read on the image: issuer and office, the duke in whose name he acts, beneficiary, legal action, property, rent terms and date. Uncertain or not read on the sheet: the issuer's surname, a short faint word read as Vos or Vor; the place in the dating clause, which is present but illegible; the amount of the rent; the street name and most of the neighbours in the bounds clause. A Konrad Vos is printed as Kellner zu Tirol in 1452, which fits the reading, but a prosopography entry names Nikolaus Gottfried as Kellner zu Tirol on 22 September 1454, so the surname is left uncertain and no reading rests on the outside name. Hermann Aichhorn, called Frank, is attested as a burgher of Meran in the 1450s, and the family "Aichhorn gen. Frank", burghers of Meran, in 1490 (Brixen, Domkapitelarchiv, Urk. 1439). No independent record of the act was found.
  • Transcription: assisted, selected lines across the whole text face. A Claude research agent read it from native-resolution crops, some contrast-stretched. Three fresh Claude readers then saw only the images and neutral questions; this was after the identification, so these are not first readings in the sense of FIRST_READINGS.md and are not preserved in readings/. Every disagreement was re-read at native resolution and either settled or marked uncertain below. The right third of the sheet is faded and water-stained. No human review; accuracy not measured.
  • Verification: there is no outside text of the act to compare. A transcription by the cataloguers is attached to the Fragmentarium record (PDF); it was read after the agent's own reading and agrees with it on almost every line, leaving the issuer's surname blank. It is a reading of this same object, not an independent record. The issuer's and beneficiary's names were checked against printed local history in Google Books snippets only. The dating clause was converted with tools/feastdate.py.

Protocol and parties

Line 1, box 1700,1230,2500,290 (image); the surname, box 2350,1270,600,150 (image):

Ich Conradt Vo[s or r] dieczeit des durchleuchtigen hochgeboren fursten vnd herren herren Sigmunden Herczogen ze Osterreich ze Steir ze Kernden vnd ze [Crain] Grave ze Tirol etc. meins gnadigen herren Chellner vnd Ambtman ze Tirol, Bekenne mit disem brieve vnd thun kunt allermenigklich, das ich anstat vnd in namen [...] mit vollem gewalt des yetzgemelten meins gnedigen herren zu erbrecht vnd pawrecht hingelassen vnd verlihen hab ...

The surname is a capital V, an o, and a long s or an r. "dieczeit" (at this time) follows.

Line 4:

dem erbern vnd weisen Herman Aichorn den man nennt Francken [...]

One or two words after the byname are faded and not read.

Property, terms and rent

Line 5, box 3350,1560,1750,140 (image):

ain haus mitsambt ainem garten gelegen an (or ze) Meran in der [street name uncertain] ...

The street name is not securely legible on the image; the attached cataloguers' transcription reads "in der Contraden wasserhalben". The bounds on four sides follow (lines 5-7); the neighbours' names are faded and not secure.

Lines 13-15:

... der yetzgenant Herman Aichorn oder sein erben dem obbemelten meinem gnedigen herren von Osterreich [...] oder [...] nachkomen in seiner gnaden Chellnerambt ze Tirol zinsen vnd geben jarleichen vnd ewigklichen zu Sannt Martens tag nach des egenanten [...] herren vrbarpuecher daselbs laut vnd sag nach herren gewonhait (?) vnd lanndsrecht der obgemelten graffschaft Tirol.

The rent amount on line 13-14, in the faded zone at about box 5300,2280,1000,160 (image), is not read. The alienation clause (line 11) limits sale to "solhen leuten ... die iren gnaden zu pawleuten an zenemen sein", and lines 16-19 add the repair duty and the Kellner's warranty.

Sealing and dating clause

Lines 20-21, box 1700,2800,5000,260 (image):

... den man nennt Francken vnd sein erben disen brief besigelt mit meinem aigem anhangenden Insigel, doch mir vnd meinen erben anschaden. G[eben ...] an Montag vor Sannt Jacobs tag des heiligen zwelfpoten Nach Cristi gepurt Tausent vierhundert vnd in dem vierundfunfczigisten Jar.

After "G[eben]" six to eight words, which held the place, are faded under staining and not read. The feast is St James the Apostle, 25 July, which in 1454 (Julian) fell on a Thursday; the Monday before is 22 July 1454. The place is not given by the image. A modern pencil note on the back reads "Meran, 1454 Juli 22."; Meran is where the property lies and where the Kellner sat, so the note is an inference.

Physical notes

  • One document of 21 long lines in German Bastarda, with a pen-drawn initial I running the height of the text, its outer stroke drawn as a fish with scale hatching. The bottom fold has a slit that may be the seal slit; the seal is lost.
  • The sheet was reused as a binding wrapper, with turn-ins on three sides and sewing holes along the top. The back carries a typed label, box 2950,2080,2200,260 on canvas 1 (image): "entnommen aus dem Einband der Wiegendruck VIII/D-9.Nr.143 / Oktober 1964 P.Maurus". F-b12f (Frg. 203, Bozen, 1492) has its own, separately typed label naming the same incunable, and its record gives that host volume. Both sheets came out of the same binding.

Catalogue corrections proposed

None has been made in Fragmentarium.

  • The act is a grant of a house and garden zu Erbrecht und Baurecht, a hereditary tenancy, not a legacy or inheritance deed: "Erbrecht" here names the tenure.
  • The record can add the regest above: issuer Konrad Vos or Vor, Kellner and Amtmann at Tirol for Duke Sigmund; beneficiary Hermann Aichhorn called Francken; property at Meran.
  • The host volume is the incunable Stams VIII/D-9 Nr. 143, removed by P. Maurus in October 1964, as for F-b12f.
  • The image labelled 01r is the back; the text is on 01v.

Unresolved

  • The issuer's surname, Vos or Vor, and whether Konrad was still Kellner in July 1454 given the September 1454 record of Nikolaus Gottfried.
  • The place in the dating clause, the rent amount, the street name, and the neighbours.
  • Searches in Google Books and the Internet Archive for the Kellner, Vos and Aichhorn found the people but not the act. A copy would most likely be in a Kellneramt register or Urbar in the Tiroler Landesarchiv, not online; monasterium.net was not searched.
