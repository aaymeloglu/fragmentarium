The Summa Lipsiensis copies the dispensation scheme but differs in the hunting and publican passages; Simon of Bisignano, Sicardus, Tractaturus magister and Huguccio were not tested. Two panels are unread, and the script looks earlier than the catalogue's fourteenth century, recorded without redating.		2026-10-12
