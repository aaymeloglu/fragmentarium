Fragmentarium: identifying the unidentified

Medieval manuscript fragments catalogued on Fragmentarium as “unidentified”, worked one at a time: read from the library’s images, searched against printed editions and digital corpora, and accepted only when consecutive lines match an edition verbatim. 22 fragments so far: 9 identified, 11 partial, 2 not identified. Each row links to the transcription and the line-by-line comparison. What remains: the burndown list of every “unidentified” record, ranked. Method in METHOD.md; what the labels mean in CONVENTIONS.md.
