Fragmentarium: identifying the unidentified

Medieval manuscript fragments catalogued on Fragmentarium as “unidentified”, worked one at a time: read from the library’s images, searched against printed editions and digital corpora, with identifications supported by sustained agreement in distinctive wording and sequence. 22 fragments so far: 8 identified, 12 partial, 2 not identified. Each row links to a report with readings, comparisons, and remaining questions. What remains: the burndown list of every “unidentified” record, ranked. Confidence refers to the identification or proposed source connection; transcription accuracy and verification coverage are separate. Research and review were performed by LLMs. Method in METHOD.md; what the labels mean in CONVENTIONS.md.
