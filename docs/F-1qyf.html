Frg. 189 (F-1qyf)

Stams, Stiftsarchiv · catalogued as x, not given

Notarial instrument: Martin Eisengrein, papal protonotary and count palatine, legitimates Johannes Vel(t)mair, son of the unmarried Wolfgang Vel(t)mair, under an inserted legatine faculty (probably the nuncio Zaccaria Delfino's, Pius IV year 6?); Ingolstadt, notary Johannes Elsner; year lost, probably 1565

The regest is read on the image: issuer Martin Eisengrein (display capitals EISENGREI[N]), protonotary of the Holy See and count palatine; beneficiary [Jo]hannes Velmair (second occurrence possibly Veltmair), son of 'Wolffgango ... soluto'; legitimation for inheritance and offices by a faculty addressed 'dilecto nobis in Christo Martino E[...]' by an envoy 'cum potestate Legati de latere' at the court of 'Maximilianum Romanorum'; given at Ingolstadt in Eisengrein's residence in the afternoon; witnesses Eustachius St[...] and Michael Aurbach, clerics; notary Johannes Elsner of Silesia, Wroclaw diocese, with his sign. The instrument's own year, day and month are lost at the cut right edge; the inserted faculty is dated at a damaged fold, read with doubt as Pius IV 'anno sexto' (1565), so 1565 is likely but supplied, and the grantor is probably Zaccaria Delfino (word 'Ph[arensis]'), though Pastor dates Delfino's appointment of Eisengrein as protonotary to 1563. A fresh reader given neutral crops confirmed the issuer, the place and hour, Maximilian, the legatine clause, the address to Martin E., 'Wolffgango ... soluto', the notary's name and sign and the witness Michael Aurbach; it read the surname as 'Velmair' and the second witness as 'Sta[...]', and could not read the fold line. No record of the legitimation was found (Google Books, MDZ, Internet Archive searched); no human review.

Readings and comparison details

F-1qyf: Martin Eisengrein, papal protonotary and count palatine, legitimates Johannes Vel(t)mair, son of Wolfgang Vel(t)mair, Ingolstadt [1565?]

This parchment sheet in the archive of the Cistercian abbey of Stams is an original notarial instrument (a deed drawn up and authenticated by a public notary) of legitimation. It is headed in display capitals "[MART]INVS EISENGREI[N]". Martin Eisengrein, councillor and "Sanctae Romanae sedis Protonotarius" (protonotary of the Holy See), records that [Jo]hannes Vel(t)mair, "genitum ex ipso dicto Wolffgango Velmair, soluto" (begotten of the said Wolfgang Velmair, an unmarried man), asked in his presence and that of the notary and witnesses to have the stain of his birth removed. The aim was that he could inherit "ex testamento vel ab intestato" (by will or on intestacy) and hold honours and offices. Eisengrein inserts the faculty under which he acts. A papal envoy "Cum potestate Legati de latere" (with the powers of a legate sent from the pope's side), at the court of "Invictissimum Principem Maximilianum Romanorum", grants "dilecto nobis in Christo Martino E[isengrein]" the dignity of count palatine. That dignity carried the power to legitimate persons "Utriusque sexus, Naturalibus, Manseribus, Spuriis, nothis, incestuosis", even after their parents' death, and to restore them to inheritance and to public and private offices. By this authority Eisengrein, "[...]ensis Comes Palatinus", declares Johannes legitimate and "habilem et idoneum" (able and fit) to receive "haereditates, Tam paternas quam maternas", and "totaliter abolemus" (wholly abolishes) the stain of birth. The act is dated "Ingolstadii in domo [...] nostrae residentiae" at "[...]unam pomeridianam" (an hour after noon), in the pontificate of "Sanctissimi in Christo patris et D[omini ...]". The witnesses are Eustachius St[...] and Michael Aurbach, clerics. The notary Johannes Elsner of Silesia, cleric of the diocese of Wrocław, subscribes with his sign. The year, day and month stood at the cut right edge and are lost. The sheet later served as the cover of a printed book.

The Fragmentarium record is Stams, Stiftsarchiv, Frg. 189, one parchment item, 365 x 206 mm (measured 345/365 x 183/206 mm), online since 2021-09-18, with the title "x" and no date, type or description. The image labelled 01v is the text face and 01r the back. Modern notes on the sheet give "Maximilian II / 1564-76" (text face) and "Einband von II, H. 32 / Ingolstat. ... / anno 1565 / D.N.D. PII papae IIII anno sexto" (back). These agree with the reading as far as the image goes. The record itself has nothing to confirm or correct beyond the missing regest (a regest is a summary of a document's issuer, parties, action and date).

  • Identification: identified, high. The regest is read on the image: the issuer (Martin Eisengrein, protonotary and count palatine), the beneficiary ([Jo]hannes Vel(t)mair, son of the unmarried Wolfgang Vel(t)mair), the action (legitimation under an inserted papal-legatine faculty), the place (Ingolstadt, Eisengrein's residence), the witnesses and the notary (Johannes Elsner, with his sign). The surname is written "Velmair" at its first, clear occurrence. At the second it may have a t ("Veltmair"), hence Vel(t)mair. The first letters of the beneficiary's given name are lost in line 5, and "Iohannes" is read later in the text. The second witness's surname is uncertain after "St". The instrument's own year, day and month are lost. A date is supplied only from the inserted faculty. Its date line lies on a fold that has destroyed most of the line, and it is read with doubt as Pius IV "anno sexto": 26 December 1564 (counting from election) or 6 January 1565 (from coronation) to Pius IV's death on 9 December 1565. The act cannot be earlier than its faculty. Eisengrein taught at Ingolstadt from 1562 and died in 1578. 1565, which the modern back note also gives, is therefore likely but not read. The grantor of the faculty is probably Zaccaria Delfino, bishop of Lesina (Hvar, in Latin "Pharensis") and papal nuncio at the imperial court until 1565. A word beginning "Ph[...]" stands in his position, and Delfino's name may be on the fold line. L. von Pastor states that Delfino made Eisengrein papal protonotary "schon im Jahre 1563". 1563 would be Pius IV's fourth year, not his sixth, so either the fold reading or the identity of the grant (a second, later faculty) is open. No outside record of this legitimation was found.
  • Transcription: the image-only first reading covers the text face, the notarial sign, the subscription and the notes on both faces. Its reader had no proposed identification or reading in context. The source-assisted reading is a research reader's (Claude) reread of the whole text face at native resolution, in six bands of about eight lines, each read in two overlapping halves, with detail crops of the display line, the beneficiary's name, the faculty's address, the fold line, the dating lines, the subscription and the sign, and of the back label and notes. It agrees with the first reading in the issuer, the father's name, Maximilian, the place and the notary. It adds the faculty's structure, the reading "Ph[...]" and the papal-year words at the fold. The fold line, the start of every line (lost at the left edge) and the ends of the dating lines remain unread. Accuracy has not been measured.
  • Verification: there is no outside text of the act to compare. A fresh reader who had not seen the reading was given neutral crops of the words the regest depends on and asked what each says. The fresh reader confirmed the display line "EISENGREI[N]", Ingolstadt "in domo ... nostre residentie", the afternoon hour, "Maximilianum", "cum potestate legati de latere", "Dilecto nobis in Christo Martino E[...]", "Wolffgango ... soluto", "Johannes [E]lsner Silesius cle(ricus)", "Signum Johannis Elsner(i)" and the witness Michael Aurbach. The fresh reader read the surname as "Velmair" (the second occurrence "Vel?mair") and the second witness as "Eustachio Sta[...]", and both are adopted above as uncertain. The fresh reader could not read the fold line, so the papal year and the grantor's name rest on the research reader alone. The dating lines are at canvas 1 (Stams_STA_Frg_189_01v), box 1150,3960-4000,4260 (image). The outside facts on Eisengrein and Delfino were read as Google Books snippets, not page images. Not checked: S. Steinherz (ed.), Nuntiaturberichte aus Deutschland II/4, Nuntius Delfino 1564-1565 (1914), and L. Pfleger, Martin Eisengrein (1908), which may print or calendar the faculty; the host book (shelf "II, H. 32"). No human review.

No edition or regest of this act was found, so there is no source column. Eisengrein's titles are given as "päpstlicher Protonotar, Pfalzgraf vom Lateran und kaiserlicher Pfalzgraf" by L. von Pastor, Erläuterungen und Ergänzungen zu Janssens Geschichte des deutschen Volkes (1908), and as "Protonotarius, Comes Palatinus Apostolicus & Caesareus" in the Annales Ingolstadiensis academiae (1580). Pastor also states that Delfino appointed him protonotary in 1563. All three were read as Google Books snippets only. A Michael Aurbach appears in the Ingolstadt university register (Die Matrikel der Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität, ed. G. von Pölnitz, 1937; snippet, date not seen). Google Books, the Munich digitisation centre's full text and the Internet Archive were searched for the beneficiary, his father, the witnesses and the notary, and gave no record of this act.

Physical notes and limits

  • One document: a display line in Roman capitals, about 33 long lines in a humanist cursive, a notarial sign at the lower left (a cross on a slanting shaft above a triangle in a rectangle, with the label band "Signum / Johannis Elsneri N[...]") and the notary's subscription of 13 lines at the lower right. The left edge is trimmed or worn, losing the first word or two of most lines. The right edge is cut, losing the last words, including the year. A horizontal fold has destroyed most of one line, and binding slits pass through the text. The seal, which the notary says Eisengrein appended, is lost with the foot of the sheet.
  • The back, canvas 0 (image), carries the remains of a paper spine label ("[...] contra / Detractores / [...]Chrysostom[i] / Epistola ad / Galatas"), modern ink notes "Einband von II, H. 32 / Ingolstat. ... / anno 1565 / D.N.D. PII papae IIII anno sexto", and pencil shelfmarks "Stams, StA, Frg. 189". The sheet was the cover of a printed theological volume, which was not identified.
  • Not claimed: the instrument's year, day and month (1565 is likely, not read), the grantor's name (Delfino is probable, not read with confidence), the mother's name, the beneficiary's origin, and the second witness's surname.
