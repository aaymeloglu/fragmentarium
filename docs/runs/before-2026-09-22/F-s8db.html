Status

IDENTIFIED — Guido de Baysio (Archidiaconus), Rosarium super Decreto, commentary on Decretum Gratiani C. 10 q. 1 c. 13 (Quia sacerdotes, Jerome to Damasus) through c. 14 (Sanctorum patrum) and the dictum opening C. 10 q. 2 (Sed cum); the other column (upper cover) is the continuation into C. 10 q. 2 (c. 1 Casellas, c. 2 Hoc ius), read only from its red initials and stray words.

Not a Glossa ordinaria: the text is a running lemma commentary with sigla ("secundum b.", "b. dicit", "Jo.", "Ricar."), which is the form of the Rosarium (c. 1300), consistent with the 14th-century dating.

Lemmata in 5–11 are all from C. 10 q. 1 c. 13 in order: orare ex offitio, comedere, concedere, vel ipsi, Papa, mittere oportet (Friedberg I 615–616). Line 20 opens the comment on c. 14 (Sanctorum patrum canonibus ... altari B. Petri et Salvatoris et S. Mariae rotundae), whose first lemma is Salvatoris. Line 59 opens the comment on the dictum of q. 2, Sed cum in episcoporum potestate ..., with the Rosarium's usual "hec questio dividitur ... in prima probat".

Siglum note: the incunable Rosarium prints give "b." (as the fragment does); Bellamera resolves the same siglum as "H." (Huguccio). Which decretist "b." denotes here (Bazianus? Bartholomaeus?) is left open.

Ruled out

  • Glossa ordinaria of Johannes Teutonicus/Bartholomaeus Brixiensis: the formula "hec tria capitula loquuntur de eadem materia" does occur in it (Google Books hits in 1550/1595 glossed Decretum editions), but none of the fragment's phrases ("presumunt accipere oblationes", "inficiebat olim romanos", "consuluit Hieronymum") does; OCR of glossed Decretum editions on archive.org (bub_gb_atwBJHXxPrYC 1595, cuadecretumgrav100xxxx 1605, bub_gb_6FqvpgJW01YC and bub_gb_ZRefrYl1A-YC 1584) searched with no hit.
  • Liber Extra / Sextus / Clementinae with Bernard of Parma / Johannes Andreae: excluded by the lemmata, which are all Decretum C. 10 q. 1 c. 13.
  • archive.org full-text search for "presumunt accipere ecclesias" (misreading of line 2–3 before the Rosarium hit): 0 hits.
