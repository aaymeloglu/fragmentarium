Frg. 81 (F-31t6)

Stams, Stiftsarchiv · catalogued as x, not given

Two manuscripts. Items 81_1-2 (hand 1): anonymous Tractatus passionis 'Scitis quia post biduum' (ed. Ferber 1935), on the arrest, the bloody sweat, the crucifixion outside the gate, Christ's age and the vinegar and gall. Hand 2: Antonius Rampegolus, Figurae Bibliae, in 81_7r (Humilitas, figura of A), 81_8r (Fides, end of A, B Docet manus meas ad prelium, opening of C), 81_9 (Religiosi, end of A and B, Expoliavi me tunica mea), 81_10r (Perseverantia, B, figura of Apoc. 1, to Exibit homo) and 81_11v (Diabolus, D, Sinite parvulos); items 81_3 and 81_4-6 and the faces 81_7v, 81_10v and 81_11r not identified

Hand 1: five passages on both faces of items 81_1 and 81_2 agree in argument, order and authorities with the commentary in Clm 2941 on page images, four collated by a research reader and 81_2r col. B by an independent checker who read it on the image before opening the source; the Stams copy has wording variants and two places of different content (a Bernard quotation on 81_2v, a note on 'Quem queritis' on 81_2r), a different redaction or copy, and Ferber's edition was not seen. Hand 2: 81_9v was collated by a research reader with the Paris 1510 print (scans 345-346, Religiosi), and an independent checker collated on page images 81_10r with scan 326 (Perseverantia), 81_8r with scans 160-161 (Fides B-C, where 'Docet manus meas ad prelium' is a lemma at scan 160 col. a) and Clm 14376 images 91-92, 81_11v with scan 129 (Diabolus D) and Clm 14376 image 72 col. b, and 81_7r with scan 189 (Humilitas). A complete CoMMA search (one batched pass, 1 November 2026) found Clm 14376 and BnF NAL 337 for 81_8r and nothing above chance for the short passages of 81_4 and 81_6. The fragment agrees with Clm 14376 against the print in some readings (insuetus, artis bellice, dum leduntur ... totus perditur fructus) and with the print against Clm in others (in pugna, Jo(b). In arte, eos vias), so its text follows neither throughout. Items 81_4-6, 81_7v, 81_10v and 81_11r are in the same hand but unplaced (81_5 too fragmentary to search), item 81_3 is unread, NAL 337 was not seen on images, the material (paper per the catalogue) was not checked, and there is no human review.

Readings and comparison details

F-31t6: Anonymous, Tractatus passionis "Scitis quia post biduum" (items 81_1-2), and Antonius Rampegolus, Figurae Bibliae, chapters Diabolus, Fides, Humilitas, Perseverantia and Religiosi (items 81_7r, 81_8r, 81_9, 81_10r, 81_11v)

This record holds pieces of two different manuscripts, which the catalogue already separates by hand. The pieces in the first hand, items 81_1 and 81_2, are from an anonymous Latin commentary on the Passion narrative of the four gospels, beginning Scitis quia post biduum Pascha fiet, edited by M. Reinhildis Ferber (1935) as the Latin source of the German Kreuziger of Johannes von Frankenstein. They cover the arrest of Christ, the bloody sweat, the crucifixion outside the gate, the age of Christ at his death, and the vinegar and gall. Five of the pieces in the second hand are from the Figurae Bibliae of Antonius Rampegolus (died 1423), an alphabetical handbook of moral topics for preachers: item 81_7r has part of the chapter Humilitas, item 81_8r part of Fides, item 81_9 part of Religiosi, item 81_10r part of Perseverantia and item 81_11v part of Diabolus. The other pieces in the second hand, items 81_4-6 and the faces 81_7v, 81_10v and 81_11r, are not placed.

The Fragmentarium record describes Stams, Stiftsarchiv, Frg. 81: paper, eleven items, 205 x 168 mm, title "x", no catalogue date and no linked description, with a reconstruction summary by Claudia Sojer assigning items 81_1-3 to one hand and 81_4-11 to another. It names no text. The six images each show several pieces mounted together: canvases 0 and 1 items 1-6 (fronts and backs), canvases 2 and 3 items 7-8, canvases 4 and 5 items 9-11. The division of hands fits the images. Items 81_1 and 81_2 are wide strips from a two-column book in gothic textura, each showing the outer column of one page, the fold, and the two columns of the facing page, so each is probably part of a bifolium (a folded sheet); item 81_3 is a small holed scrap. Items 81_4-11 are in a small, fast, heavily abbreviated cursive with red initials. The catalogue says paper; the first reading took the pieces for parchment; the material was not checked.

  • Identification: identified, high, for items 81_1-2 (hand 1) and 81_7r, 81_8r, 81_9, 81_10r and 81_11v (hand 2). Items 81_3 and 81_4-6, and the faces 81_7v, 81_10v and 81_11r, are not identified.
    • Hand 1: five passages, on both faces of item 81_1 and both faces of item 81_2, agree with the commentary in Munich, BSB, Clm 2941 in argument, order of questions, authorities (Hebrews, Chrysostom, Isaiah 53:7, "dominus Albertus", Theophilus) and transitions, between red-framed lemmata (quoted gospel phrases) such as "Sequitur. Dormite" and "Sequitur. In littera". The fragment carries the commentary itself, not a quotation from it. The Stams copy differs from Clm 2941 in wording ("intimus" for "intrinsecus", "naturalem" for "supernaturalem", "universo mundo" for "toti et universo orbi") and twice in content: on 81_2v a boxed quotation of Bernard (O Maria cur filius ...) follows the bloody sweat where Clm passes to Adhuc eo loquente, and on 81_2r a note on "Quem queritis" follows the Isaiah quotation where Clm has Augustine on the falling back. This tract circulates in several versions; these are differences of redaction or copy, not of work. Thomas Aquinas, Summa theologiae III q. 46 a. 10, shares the phrase "ut veritas responderet figurae", but the surrounding text is not his.
    • Hand 2: on item 81_9 the end of section A of Religiosi (the three strong men at the cistern of Bethlehem, 2 Sam 23) and section B (Expoliavi me tunica mea, Song of Songs 5, with the figura, the Old Testament type, of Jonathan giving his tunic to David, 1 Sam 18) agree with the Paris 1510 print in sequence of lemma, moralisation and figura. On item 81_10 section B of Perseverantia (the figura of Apocalypse 1, the Son of Man vestitum podere) runs into the opening of the next section, Exibit homo ad opus suum, as in the print. Differences are of wording: "veste propria se exuens" for "vestem propriam extrahens", "attingens" for "pertingens", "tui certaminis obtinere" for "certaminis tui habere". On item 81_8r the end of section A of Fides, all of section B (lemma Docet manus meas ad prelium, Ps 143, on the training of soldiers, with Vegetius) and the opening of section C (Notas mihi fecisti vias vite, Ps 15) follow the print, scans 160-161, in order. On item 81_11v section D of Diabolus (Sinite parvulos venire ad me, Mt 19, on plants harmed in flower) follows scan 129, and on item 81_7r the figura of Joseph and Benjamin that closes section A of Humilitas follows scan 189.
    • A manuscript of the work, Munich, BSB, Clm 14376, was compared for 81_8r and 81_11v. The fragment agrees with it against the print in some readings: "insuetus" (print "inconsuetus"), "artis bellice" ("in artibus bellicis"), "clamorem hostium" ("hostium clamores"), "Satis bene facit" ("Satis ... facit"), "eos" ("eis"), and "dum leduntur in floribus totus perditur fructus" ("si ledantur in floribus totus eius fructus sequens leditur"). It agrees with the print against Clm 14376 in others: "in pugna" (Clm "in bello"), "Jo(b). In arte" (Clm "in marte", without Job), "eos vias" (Clm without "vias"). The fragment's text therefore follows neither witness throughout; these are differences of copy, not of work.
    • Not identified: items 81_4-6 and the faces 81_7v, 81_10v and 81_11r are in the same hand, with lemmata under red initials and "Fig(ura)" sections, but were not placed in the print. A search of the CoMMA corpus (machine-read text of about 32,000 digitised manuscripts) found nothing above chance for the passages of 81_4r, 81_4v and 81_6v, which are short and mostly from the first reading; 81_5 has only line fragments of one to three words and was not searched; 81_7v was not located in the print. They may come from the same work, but that is not shown.
  • Transcription: the image-only first reading and the source-assisted reading. The first reading did not cover canvases 2, 3 and 5. A research reader reread on the native images 81_1r col. B (lower) and col. C (upper), 81_1v col. B, 81_2v col. B (upper) and two bands of 81_9v, all with the source in hand, and later 81_7r, 81_8r sections B and C, and 81_11v, mostly before opening the print. An independent checker then read 81_7r, 81_8r sections B and C, and 81_11v on the images before opening each source page, though after seeing the research reader's transcription, so these readings are not blind; its lines are the ones given in the tables below. An independent checker read 81_2r col. B, lines 1-21, and the upper part of 81_10r before opening the sources, and the lower part of 81_10r after (see Verification); its lines are given in the tables below, not in the assisted file. The reading of 81_9r rests on the first reading. The hand-2 cursive is hard to read, and the readings of 81_9v and 81_10r are reliable mainly in the phrases compared. Accuracy has not been measured.
  • Verification: a research reader compared four hand-1 passages (81_1r cols. B and C, 81_1v col. B, 81_2v col. B) with page images of Clm 2941, images 397, 416 and 417, and the hand-2 passages of 81_9v with page images of the Paris 1510 print, scans 345-346. An independent checker read 81_2r col. B, lines 1-21 (canvas 0, native box 5050,2580,950,1370), a face not collated by the research reader, on the image before opening the source, and compared it with the page image of Clm 2941, image 397, col. b: lines 1-17 follow Clm argument by argument, with the same authorities and the same two causes, and the text diverges after line 17. For hand 2 it read the upper part of 81_10r (canvas 4, native boxes 5000,2700,1720,750 and 5000,3400,1720,750) on the image before opening any source, found the page through the MDZ full text of the print on its anchor phrases ("podere", "talos", "fidelis"), and compared it with the page image of the Paris 1510 print, scan 326, chapter Perseverantia: the figura, the moralisation, the authorities and the next lemma agree in order, with the variants given below. A search of the CoMMA corpus, run complete in one batched pass on 1 November 2026, returned for the 81_8r passage two manuscripts of the Figurae Bibliae, Paris, BnF, NAL 337, and Munich, BSB, Clm 14376, both with the same text; the other hand-2 passages gave nothing above chance. An independent checker confirmed these on page images: 81_8r (canvas 2, native box 1150,1250,1750,850) against the print, scan 160, where Docet manus meas ad prelium is the lemma of section B of Fides in col. a, and scan 161, and against Clm 14376, images 91 (col. b) and 92 (col. a); 81_11v (canvas 5, native box 1000,1480,1800,500) against scan 129, section D of Diabolus, and Clm 14376, image 72, col. b; and 81_7r (canvas 2, native box 5250,1400,1700,700) against scan 189, Humilitas. Gaps: Ferber's edition was seen only through a search inside its Google Books volume, so the hand-1 text was compared with one manuscript, not the edition; BnF NAL 337 was not read on its images; items 81_4-6 and the faces 81_7v, 81_10v and 81_11r were not placed, and 81_3 was not examined; 81_2r after line 17 and the lower part of 81_10r were read only with the source in hand. No person has reviewed the reading or the match.

BnF NAL 337, the other manuscript the search returned, was seen only in its machine-read text.

Other items and limits

Items 81_4-6 and the faces 81_7v, 81_10v and 81_11r (hand 2) are not placed. Lemmata read on them, mostly in the first reading: 81_6v Psalm 106:26 Ascendunt usque ad celos; 81_7v a section on kin ("cognatio") under a red C; 81_10v Sagitte potentis acute; 81_11r a numbered list ("6o vestit vasa ad sacrificium ...", "palleum"). Searches of the 1510 print's full text did not find these as lemmata, but its OCR is unreliable (it misses the lemma of 81_8r), and the CoMMA misses on short passages are not negative results either. The Figura Exodi at the foot of 81_8r section C was read only with the print in hand and is not in the tables. Item 81_3 is assigned to hand 1 by the catalogue only and is unread. Item 81_2r beyond the checker's lines, with Papias and a figura of Saul, was not collated.

Not claimed: that items 81_4-6 and the faces 81_7v, 81_10v and 81_11r are from the Figurae Bibliae; that the hand-2 pieces come from one codex; any relation of the Stams copy to Clm 14376 or the 1510 print beyond the readings listed; which redaction of the Passion commentary the hand-1 pieces follow; the material, date or origin of either manuscript; or the arrangement of the bifolia.
