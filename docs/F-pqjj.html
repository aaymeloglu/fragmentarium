Readings and comparison details

F-pqjj: Emperor Rudolf II grants arms with the Lehenartikel to Georg Jäger, father of the abbot of Stams, 1601 (unfinished exemplar)

This parchment sheet in the archive of the Cistercian abbey of Stams is an unfinished exemplar of an imperial grant of arms. Emperor Rudolf II grants Georg Jäger, as father of the reigning abbot of Stams, together with his legitimate heirs and their heirs forever, a coat of arms with the Lehenartikel (the clause admitting the bearers to the status of persons capable of holding fiefs). The arms: a narrow bend of white and red lozenges set "Sachsweis"; a tilting helm with mantling blue and white on the left and red and white on the right; as crest a white greyhound with a gilded collar, springing. The dating clause gives the 26th year of Rudolf's Roman reign and, probably, the year 1601; the register of grants dates the act Prague, 18 June 1601.

The same grant survives at Stams on a second unfinished sheet in another hand and wording, F-fc4z (Frg. 202), which keeps the place and the month. The two records carry one act.

The Fragmentarium record is Stams, Stiftsarchiv, Frg. 201, catalogued as "Deed", German, Prague, 1601, 361 x 291 mm, removed from the Urbar STB VIII/21.

Protocol and narration

The display line (about 1700,750,2200,500) is faded almost to nothing; only outlines of large letters remain, and the issuer's name is not legible. Box 1500,1380,5100,180 (image) for the abbot clause.

[...] zu allen zeiten Merer des Reichs in Germanien, zu Hungern, Behaim, Dalmatien, Cro[atien ...] zu Crain, zu Württemberg [...] Ober vnnd Nider Schlesien, Fürst zu Schwaben, Marggraue [...] Tyrol, zu Pfirdt, zu Kyburg vnnd zu Görtz, Landtgraue in Elsass, Herr [zu der] Windischen Marckh, zu Porten[au ...] Das Wir gnedigclich angesehen, [wahrge]nomen vnnd betracht die Erbarkait, Redlichait, auch [...] getreuen, aufrechten, vleissigen vnnd willigen dienste [...] als ain gehorsamer Tyrolischer Lanndtman [...] der jetzt Regierender Abbt dess Gotzhaus Stambs, vnnd [...] fürnembsten Prelaten ainer vnnserer Fürstlichen Graffschafft [Tyrol ...]

"Abbt dess Gotzhaus Stambs" is secure. The line starts where the son's name would stand are damaged; "Melchior" is not legible on this sheet.

Blazon and beneficiary

Box 1550,1840,5100,700 (image).

[...] derselben Erbens Erben für vnnd für in ewig zeit, diß hernachgeschriben Wappen vnnd Clainot. Mit namen [...] ain schmale Strassen, in weisse vnnd Rotte Rauten Sachsweis außgesetzt, ob vnnd vnnder derselben aber erscheinen[d ...] ain Steechhelm, die Linckhen mit blaw, Rechten Rott vnnd [...] [baiders]eits weissen Helmdeckhen, vnnd darob ainer [...] außwerts kerent, vnnd oben das Linckh in [...] vnnd weisse, das Recht [Horn] aber in Rott [...] tail ainer weissen Windspils mit übergultem Halßbanndt zum Sprung geschickht, Als [in mitte dis]es Briefs gemahlet vnnd mit farben aigentlicher außgestrichen ist, von Newem gnedig[clich ...] der obgenannt Georg Jäger, seine Eheliche Leibs Erben vnnd derselben Erbens Erben [...]

A blank field on the left (about 1600,2050,850,1650) is left for the painted arms that the blazon refers to; it was never painted, and the lines beside it are indented. The Lehenartikel follows in its usual form ("in Insiglen, Petschafften, Clainoten, Begräbnussen [...] Wappen vnnd Lechens[genossen ...]"), read in outline.

Penalty and dating clause

Box 2400,4100,3700,320 (image).

[...] in vnnser vnnd des Reichs Camer, vnnd den anndern [halben tail] dem vilbenannten Georg Jäger, sein[en ...] [...] geben ist auf vnnserm Künig[c]lichen [...] Schloß(?) [...] Se[chzehen]hundert(?) vnnd im Erst[en](?), [vnserer Reiche] des Römischen im Sechsundzwainzig[isten] [cut]

"vnnserm Künig[c]lichen" and "Römischen im Sechsundzwainzig[isten]" are secure. The place name is not visible, and the day and month are illegible in the faded, water-stained line. The year in words is probable only: "Se" at the start and "...dert vnnd im Er..." are visible, the middle letters faded. The Hungarian and Bohemian years are lost at the cut lower edge.

Dating

Rudolf II was elected King of the Romans on 27 October 1575, and his chancery counted the Roman regnal years from the election, so the 26th Roman year ran from 27 October 1600 to 26 October 1601. The probable year in words, 1601, falls in it. Place, day and month are supplied from Frank: Prague, 18 June 1601. F-fc4z, the other exemplar, reads Prague and June; its day is illegible.

Physical notes and catalogue

  • Neither this sheet nor F-fc4z is the engrossment handed to the beneficiary. Both leave the field for the painted arms blank, both have struck-out words (here in the corroboration area, about 3800,3560,600,50, with an interlinear correction), and neither has a plica, seal or signature; the lower edge is cut through the regnal-year line. Drafts or rejected fair copies that stayed at Stams are the likely explanation; nothing on the sheets documents it.
  • The hand is a formal upright chancery script, not the cursive of F-fc4z.
  • The back carries the host volume's cover title, "[Vrba]r(?) | [...]hs Ambts auf | Anno 1.6.12. Jar", the shelf label VIII/21, and tie slits along the spine fold like those on F-fc4z, whose cover title also names 1612. The two sheets were probably reused as covers for the same set of Urbare.
  • Proposed catalogue corrections (Fragmentarium not updated): the document is an unfinished exemplar of an imperial grant of arms with the Lehenartikel for Georg Jäger, 1601 (18 June by Frank); the image labelled 01r is the back of the sheet and the text is on 01v.
