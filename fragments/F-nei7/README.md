In the counting construction in v.3–4, the abbreviated participle remains tentative, but **cum predictis** and the counts are visible. The text distinguishes what innascibility names negatively from what it allows one to understand positively. This requires more care than assigning the author to a school from “negation-only” vocabulary.

The internal references include *supra d.18*, *infra d.26*, and, at r.39, **“Sed hoc magis spectat ad 2m librum.”** These support a *Sentences* context. They do not securely locate this leaf in a particular distinction: references may anticipate or recall discussions without mapping the present passage to a narrow numbered range. Verso 6–9 are not included in this transcription; only **“Circa 2m principale”** is retained from the underlined v.10.

The supported result is an anonymous theological discussion in a probable *Sentences* I context, **Partial / low**.
