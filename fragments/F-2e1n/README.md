- (Cieo cont.: "... Immo citum. Item cio et cieo eandem habent significationem, s. vocare, movere, amovere, congregare. Cieo cies tamen accipitur in alia significatione, s. festinare, sperare, in qua non accipitur cio cis. Cieo et cio componuntur cum ad et dicitur accieo cies et accio cis i. appellare, advocare. - *Cigneus* -nea -neum i. candidus vel ad cignum pertinens vel de cigno existens, et dicitur a cignus.
- *Cignitus* -ti masculini generis, vox cigni, a cignus dicitur, et producit gni.
- *Cignus*: ab hoc ... dicitur hic cignus -gni, quia bene cantat. Unde hic cignulus -li diminutivum, parvus cignus; et cigninus -na -num, et cigneus -nea -neum. Item a cignus dictus est amnis quidam Cilicie hic cignus, tum quia miram habeat aquarum suavitatem, tum quia autumno et estate cum nives solvuntur tumescit. Quedam enim lingua quicquid est candidum dicit cignum ... Reliqua vero ... tenuis est et quietus, secundum Hugutionem. Papias autem sic dicit: cignus avis genus que et olor dicitur, quia totus sit albus plumis, a canendo dictus, eo quod carmina modulantes vocibus fundat. Item cignus Cilicie fluvius.

- *Cilia* venter interpretatur, unde ciliacus qui in ventre habet passionem, et dicitur celia

- *Cilices* quidam ... (Cilicia ...) ... piratae ... "ponuntur cilices pro piratis et pirate pro cilicibus"

- *Cilicium* -cii ... vestimentum factum de pilis caprarum vel ... tegumentum ... et dicitur a Cilicia

## Notes
- Date/script are consistent: the Catholicon (1286) was copied in huge numbers in the 14th–15th c.; a two-column textualis copy of c. 1400–1450 in Bruges fits. Nothing in the text contradicts the catalogue. The running "C" headers and alternating red/blue initials are standard Catholicon layout.
- The fragment's readings have minor variants against the 1475 print (e.g. "Catapuera docta puella" matches; "cataposis" in the fragment where the print reads "catatiposis"; fragment "Cicendelum" vs print "Cicendulum"). These are ordinary manuscript variants, not a different text.
- The 1460 Mainz Catholicon (archive.org OEXV2, Bibliothèque Sainte-Geneviève OEXV 2 RES) also exists in OCR but the OCR is unusable for these leaves; the Rusch print was used instead.

## Ruled out
- Isidore, Etymologiae (not alphabetical; entries here cite Hugutio, Papias, Pantheon, Theodolus, Graecismus, which post-date Isidore).
- Papias, Elementarium; Hugutio, Derivationes; Osbern; Brito; Vocabularius Ex quo; Gerardus de Schueren: excluded because the fragment itself cites "secundum Hugutionem", "secundum Papiam", "in Pantheon" and "in iiii. parte dixi" (the Catholicon's own cross-reference to its fourth part, on tropes and rhetorical colours) and "de isto libro sicut dixi in principio" under *Catholicon*; and because the wording is verbatim Balbus.
