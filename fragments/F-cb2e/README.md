# F-cb2e: Henricus de Frimaria (as listed by Schneyer), *Sermones de tempore*, series 2, Septuagesima to the third Sunday after Easter

The six paper bifolia (sheets folded to make two leaves) and one single leaf of this record come from a copy of a de tempore sermon collection, that is, sermons for the Sundays and feasts of the church year. The sermons are short, and after the theme (the opening Bible verse) each begins "Nota quod ...". J. B. Schneyer's *Repertorium der lateinischen Sermones des Mittelalters* lists the series under Henricus de Frimaria (Heinrich von Friemar), an Augustinian friar of Erfurt, as his *Sermones de tempore*, series 2. The surviving sermons run from Septuagesima to the third Sunday after Easter (Jubilate), with Ash Wednesday, the first, fourth and fifth Sundays of Lent, Palm Sunday, Easter Sunday and Easter week between. Compared with the copy in Munich, BSB, Clm 3764, the Stams text is an abridged form of the series.

The [Fragmentarium record](https://fragmentarium.ms/overview/F-cb2e) describes Stams, Stiftsarchiv, Frg. 80: paper, seven fragment items, 297 x 182 mm (measured on Frg. 80_2), with a reconstruction summary by Claudia Sojer, no catalogue date, title "x" and no linked description; its fourteen images are labelled 01r to 07v. It does not name the text. Its description fits the images: six bifolia (items 01 and 03-07) and one single leaf (item 02), paper with chain lines, written in two columns of about 26-28 lines a page in a late gothic cursive, with red rubrics, red touches on initials and red underlining of citations.

- **Identification:** identified, high, for the sermon series; the author is as Schneyer lists it.
  - Basis: the sermon openings and closings (explicits) read on canvases 0-8 agree with Schneyer's incipits and explicits for nos. 160-163, 170 and 175-180, in his order; and three body-text passages agree with Clm 3764, sentence by sentence: the end of the Septuagesima sermon (1r col. d), the end of Septuagesima and the start of Sexagesima (1v col. a), and the end of the Ash Wednesday sermon (06r col. c). The run of themes, the "Nota quod" openings with their threefold divisions, and the same authorities in the same places favour the series itself over a borrowing of single sermons.
  - Version: the Stams text is shorter than Clm 3764 throughout the compared passages, consistently and in one direction: it lacks "tale", "Christus vel", "unigeniti" and the clause "fecit erigi crucem sanctam" in the first two passages, and "virtutes", "et velat quia ... et conservat", "et quorum" and "bene" in the third, and it adds one sentence ("Aspergamus ergo nos cinere ...") that Clm lacks there. So it is an abridged form of the series, or Clm 3764 has a fuller one; which is the case is not determined.
  - Attribution: Schneyer places the series under Henricus de Frimaria, and Clm 3764's old table of contents names "Sermones henrici de frimaria erfordensis conuentus Augustinorum". Two Augustinians of that name are known, the elder (died 1340) and Henricus de Frimaria iunior; which of them wrote this series is not established.
- **Transcription:** the [image-only first reading](https://github.com/aaymeloglu/fragmentarium/blob/main/fragments/F-cb2e/readings/stams4-2026-10-31-image-only.json) and the [source-assisted reading](https://github.com/aaymeloglu/fragmentarium/blob/main/fragments/F-cb2e/readings/stams4-2026-10-31-source-assisted.json). A research reader reread on the native images 1r col. d (canvas 0), 1v col. a (canvas 1), the tops of col. c on canvases 1 and 5, and fifteen sermon openings and explicits around the red initials and rubrics of canvases 2-13. An independent checker read 06r col. c, lines 1-19 (canvas 10), and the last line of 1v col. b and the first line of 03r col. c (see Verification). The end of no. 177 and the opening of no. 178 on 1r col. a, and 1v col. b, rest on the first reading. The body text of bifolia 04-07, apart from the checker's passage, and the stained middle of canvas 1, cols. b-d, are unread. Accuracy has not been measured.
- **Verification:** a research reader collated 1r col. d and 1v col. a with page images of Clm 3764, ff. 200v-201r, and compared the sermon openings with Schneyer's entries, which were seen only as Google Books snippets. An independent checker read 06r col. c, lines 1-19 (canvas 10, native box 4400,560,1200,2000), a face the research reader had not read, on the image before opening the source, then compared it with the [page image of Clm 3764, f. 202v (scan 416)](https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb00114305?page=416), col. a, lower half. It found the end of the Ash Wednesday sermon *Aspergere cinere* (Jer 6) matching sentence by sentence, with the same Augustine and Bernard citations and Psalm 31 *Beati quorum remisse*, in the shortened form described above; six words it had read wrongly or uncertainly before seeing the source resolved to Clm readings that the letterforms allow. It identified two more sermons on that face, *Cum ieiunasset* for the first Sunday of Lent (col. d), matching Clm 3764 f. 202v col. b at overview size, and the Easter Sunday sermon *Surrexit, non est hic* (col. b). It also confirmed that bifolium 03 lies directly inside bifolium 01 by a verbal join (see below). Gaps: Schneyer's printed pages, the Lübeck manuscript and the Clm 3764 catalogue description were not seen; Schneyer numbers were not checked for the Lent and Easter Sunday sermons; Clm 3764 is a low-resolution black-and-white microfilm scan; the attribution was not reviewed beyond Schneyer and the Clm table of contents. No person has reviewed the reading or the match.

Apart from the abridgement described under Identification, the differences are of wording and order: "Mt 22" where Clm has "xxi" (Mt 21:33 is correct), "Sed cum" for "sicut ergo ... sic et ecclesia", "Et figuratum est" for "Unde figuratur", "diliguntur" (uncertain) for "colliguntur", "deus" for "dominus", "illam" for "istam", and "hodie predicatores quibus ipse dicit Ite", which leads into the Septuagesima theme. Witness: Munich, BSB, Clm 3764, ff. 200v-201r and 202v, [MDZ bsb00114305](https://mdz-nbn-resolving.de/urn:nbn:de:bvb:12-bsb00114305-7), scans [412](https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb00114305?page=412), [413](https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb00114305?page=413) and [416](https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb00114305?page=416), read as page images of a black-and-white microfilm. Its f. 202r has *Sequebatur eum laudans deum* (no. 163) before the Ash Wednesday sermon, the same order as in Stams.

## Leaf order, other items, and limits

Bifolium 03 lies directly inside bifolium 01. The text runs from the Septuagesima sermon on 1r cols. c-d to 1v cols. a-b (end of Septuagesima, Sexagesima) and on to 03r cols. c-d (Quinquagesima at col. d). The join is verbal: the last line of 1v col. b (canvas 1, 2900,3500,1300,500) reads "... Ps. Nolite fieri" and the first line of 03r col. c (canvas 4, 4400,700,1400,500) reads "sicut equus et mulus in quibus non est intellectus, id est racio", Psalm 31:9 running from leaf to leaf. On the other half of the sheets, 03r col. b ends with the theme *Erant simul Petrus et Johannes*; that sermon continues at the head of 1v col. c and ends on 1r col. a, followed by *Quorum remiseritis*. The single leaf 02 continues from there, with the end of no. 178, no. 179 and the start of no. 180. Only the 1v/03r join was checked word by word. Bifolium 06 has Ash Wednesday and the first Sunday of Lent on one half and the Easter Sunday sermon on the other, so it is an inner sheet of the Lent and Easter run. The nesting of bifolia 04-07 is not reconstructed.

Not claimed: which Henricus de Frimaria wrote the series, whether the Stams text is an abridgement or Clm 3764 an expansion, the date and origin of the Stams copy, or the content of the unread body text.
