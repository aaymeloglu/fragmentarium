# F-ns7l: Cristan Platter auf dem Haunoldt exchanges his share of the Haunoldt farm, held under Stams abbey, with Sebastian Kurtz and his wife, 17 June [before 1592]

This parchment sheet in the archive of the Cistercian abbey of Stams carries the upper part of one deed of exchange (Tauschbrief), issued in the first person by Cristan Platter: "Ich Cristan Platter Auf dem Haunoldt, im Ge[richt ...]". With the knowledge and consent of his court lords and ground lords (Grundherren, the lords to whom a holding owes rent), Platter makes "ainen Tausch vnnd wechßl" (an exchange) in the princely county of Tyrol with the married couple ("Eewirtleut") Sebastian Kurtz and A[...]. He hands over into perpetual possession his share of the whole Haunoldt farm ("deß ganntzen Haunoldt hofs"), with house, farmstead and outbuildings, which came from "meinem leiblichen lieben vattern seligen Thoman Platter". The ground rents of the "Gotßhaus Stambs" (Stams abbey) on the farm are reserved. In return the couple hand over to Platter all the property they held until then, and a balancing sum "zuuergleichung" (to equalise the exchange) is paid in gulden "zu Dreyßig kreitzer guetter Tyrolischer lanndtswerung" (Tyrolean local currency, reckoned at thirty kreuzer). The Kurtz couple's property is described as farm, farmstead, barn, stable, kitchen garden, bake-oven, fields, gardens, meadows, water and water channels, cultivated and uncultivated land, with nothing excepted. The "Grundherrschafft zu Stambs" consents, and Platter gives the warranty "wie ... lanndtsrecht ist" (as the law of the land requires). A sealer, "herrn Hann[s]en (?) [...]", is asked to seal, and the witnesses to that request follow ("Siglpittung seind gezeu[gen]"). The deed is dated "Beschehen den Sibenzehenden tag Monats Junij · Nach Christ[i ...]" (17 June). The year, the amount of the sum, the court district and the sealer's full name stood in the lost right part of the lines. The sheet was later cut down to serve as the cover of a Stams register labelled "Ao 92".

The [Fragmentarium record](https://fragmentarium.ms/overview/F-ns7l) is Stams, Stiftsarchiv, Frg. 50, one parchment item, 235 x 219 mm (measured 230/235 x 213/219 mm), online since 2021-09-18, with the title "x" and no date, type or description. The image labelled 01r is the text face and 01v the back. The record has nothing to confirm or correct beyond the missing regest (a regest is a summary of a document's issuer, parties, action and date).

- **Identification:** identified, high. The regest is read on the image: the issuer (Cristan Platter), the other party (the married couple Sebastian Kurtz and A[...]), the action (exchange of Platter's share of the Haunoldt farm, inherited from his late father Thoman Platter, for the couple's holding plus a balancing sum), the ground lord (Stams abbey, whose rents are reserved and whose consent is recorded) and the day of issue (17 June). The farm name is clear as "Haunoldt" in the display line. In the cursive of line 5 the same name could be read "Haimoldt", but the display form is preferred. **Lost at the cut right edge:** the year, the amount of the sum, the court district ("im Ge[richt ...]"), the wife's name, the place of the couple's holding, and the sealer's surname and office. **The year is supplied as before [15]92, not read:** the trimmed sheet became the cover of a register dated "Ao 92" on its back, most likely 1592. The hand, the spelling and the gulden-kreuzer reckoning fit the sixteenth century. A Cristan Platter and a Thoman Platter, both "schmid zu Etz" (smiths at Ötz), are listed in *Quellen zur Geschichte der Täufer*, Österreich, ed. G. Mecenseffy (1972). A Sebastian Kurz, "Dorfmeister zu Mötz" (village headman of Mötz), appears in *Die Gemeindearchive des Bezirkes Imst* (1995). Ötz and Mötz both lay in the court district of St. Petersberg near Stams, so these men may be the parties of this deed. Both sources were seen as snippets only, and the years of those entries were not seen, so they are leads and not records of this act.
- **Transcription:** the [image-only first reading](https://github.com/aaymeloglu/fragmentarium/blob/main/fragments/F-ns7l/readings/stams-2026-10-31-image-only.json) covers the display line, all 23 text lines and the back. Its reader had no proposed identification or reading in context. The [source-assisted reading](https://github.com/aaymeloglu/fragmentarium/blob/main/fragments/F-ns7l/readings/stams-2026-10-31-source-assisted.json) is a research reader's (Claude) reread of the whole text face at native resolution, in six bands of about four lines, each read in two overlapping halves, with detail crops of the farm name, the father's name, the parties, the currency, the sealing clause and the dating line, and of the back heading. It agrees with the first reading in the names and the date. It reads "Eewirtleut" (married couple) where the first reading had "Hauswirtlain", and "vberantwort" (handed over) at the start of line 9. The sealer's name in line 22, the word before "mein Platters" in line 18 and some descriptive words in lines 9 and 14 remain uncertain. Accuracy has not been measured.
- **Verification:** there is no outside text of the act to compare. A fresh reader who had not seen the reading was given neutral crops of the words the regest depends on and asked what each says. The fresh reader confirmed "Cristan Platter", "Auf dem Haunoldt" in the display line (with line 5 looking like "haim?odt"), "Thoman Platter", "Gotshaus [St]ambs" with "Grundtzinsen", "Sebastian Kurtz", "Dreissig kreitzer guetter Tyrolischer lanndtswerung", "Sibentzehenden tag Monats Junij". The fresh reader saw no year on the sheet. A research reader reread the back heading as "[Ke]llerambts Vrbar Ao 92". The dating line (line 23) is at canvas 0 (Stams_STA_Frg_050_01r), box 2800,3440-5300,3580 ([image](https://fragmentarium.ms/loris/F-ns7l/Stams_STA_Frg_050_01r.jpg/2800,3440,2500,140/full/0/default.jpg)). Not checked: the host register (pencil "Hs. 122") and other Stams registers of the 1590s, which may name the farm and its holders, and the Tiroler Landesarchiv records of the court of St. Petersberg. No human review.

## Physical notes and limits

- One document of a display line and 23 lines in a German chancery cursive, with a large calligraphic initial I whose shafts rise above the display line and a cadel flourish down the left margin. The right part of the sheet is cut away, losing the end of every line, and the foot with the seal is gone. The blank lower part of the face was the turned-in part of the later cover.
- The back carries the cover title of the host register, box 4050,1000-5600,1700 on canvas 1 ([image](https://fragmentarium.ms/loris/F-ns7l/Stams_STA_Frg_050_01v.jpg/4050,1000,1550,700/full/0/default.jpg)): "[Ke]llerambts / Vrbar, A(nn)o 92", with a large paraph. That makes it a rent register (Urbar) of an abbey office, probably the cellarer's. Modern additions are a pencil "III/16", a pencil "Hs. 122" (probably the host register's shelfmark, not checked), the stamp "Stiftsarchiv Stams" and the pencil "Stams, STA, Frg. 50v". Faint offset of other writing shows at the lower left.
- Not claimed: the year (only a terminus before 1592, inferred from the reuse), the amount of the sum, the court district, the location of the Haunoldt farm, the wife's name, the sealer's identity, and the identity of the parties with the Ötz and Mötz men named in the printed leads.
