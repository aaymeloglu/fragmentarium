# F-xyan: Hans Trautson, as guardian, grants Martin Wenig (or Rennig) and Bartlme, grandson of Hans Capler, a field at Lienz in fief, Innsbruck, 2 June 1545

This sheet in the archive of the Cistercian abbey of Stams is the original of a fief grant (Lehenbrief). Hans Trautson, Freiherr zu Sprechenstein, Obersthofmarschall of King Ferdinand, acting for himself and in the stead of his co-guardian Anton von Brandis zu Leonburg, in the name of their cousin and ward, grants Martin Wenig or Rennig of the Landgericht Lienz, for himself and as Lehenträger for Bartlme, grandson of the late Hans Capler of Patriasdorf, a field at Lienz above the parish church as a proper fief. The fief had fallen to them by inheritance on Hans Capler's death. Trautson's pendant seal was announced. The sheet later served as the cover of a Stams account book of 1549.

The [Fragmentarium record](https://fragmentarium.ms/overview/F-xyan) is Stams, Stiftsarchiv, Frg. 200, catalogued as "Urkunde / Deed", German, 1525, 338 x 246 mm, host volume STB 213, dated 1549.

- **Identification:** identified, high. The regest is read on the image: issuer, co-guardian and capacity, beneficiaries, legal action, object and bounds, place and date. Uncertain on the sheet: the beneficiary's surname (Wenig or Rennig) and a few words in the bounds clause. The ward is not named in the text; Trautson and Brandis are recorded as guardians of Christof von Wolkenstein, son of Veit von Wolkenstein, lord of Lienz (died 1538), which suggests who he is, but that is an inference, not a reading. No independent record of this grant was found; the likely place for one, a Lienz or Wolkenstein fief register in the Tiroler Landesarchiv, is not online.
- **Transcription:** assisted, the whole text face. A Claude research agent read it from native-resolution crops, with historical context in hand. Three fresh Claude readers then saw only the images and neutral questions; this was after the identification, so these are not first readings in the sense of [FIRST_READINGS.md](../../FIRST_READINGS.md) and are not preserved in `readings/`. Every disagreement was re-read at native resolution and either settled or marked uncertain below. No human review; accuracy not measured.
- **Verification:** there is no outside text of the act to compare. The year, the issuers and their offices were checked against internal evidence and printed context: Ferdinand's Roman, Hungarian and Bohemian titles, which he held from 1526 and 1531, fit 1545; Trautson's office and the Trautson-Brandis guardianship are attested in Google Books snippets (sources below). No page images of those sources were read.

### Protocol and parties

Opening, box `2200,850,4100,300` ([image](https://fragmentarium.ms/loris/F-xyan/Stams_STA_Frg_200_01v.jpg/2200,850,4100,300/full/0/default.jpg)):

> Ich Hanns Trautson Freyherr zu Sprechenstain, Römischer, Hungarischer vnnd Behamischer Küniglicher Maiestat etc. meins allergnedigisten herren, Obrister Hofmarschalkh etc. Bekhenn offenlich mit disem brief, Das vor mir Erschinen ist, Martin Wenig (or Rennig) im Landtgericht Lüenz gesessen, für sich selbs vnnd als verordneter Lehentrager Barthlome, weilend Hansen Caplers seligen zu Patriasdorf (?) im Landtgericht Lüenz Enikhel

Beneficiary's surname, box `3150,1150,800,110` ([image](https://fragmentarium.ms/loris/F-xyan/Stams_STA_Frg_200_01v.jpg/3150,1150,800,110/full/0/default.jpg)). The capital has the crossed form the scribe also uses for R; the following letters are e, three or four minims, and "ig". Wenig and Rennig are both possible.

Co-guardian and ward, box `1950,1550,4350,210` ([image](https://fragmentarium.ms/loris/F-xyan/Stams_STA_Frg_200_01v.jpg/1950,1550,4350,210/full/0/default.jpg)):

> Vnnd hie mich auch anstatt des Edlen vnnd Vesten Anthonien von Brandis zu Leonburg Hof[maister or khamrer] (?) Küniglicher Maiestat Rats vnnd Regenten meins mitvormunders, In namen vorgemelts vnsers Vettern vnnd Pflegsuns

The reason for the inheritance reads "nach absterben Erstgedachts Hansen Caplers seines Vettern (or Vattern)", so whether Martin was Capler's kinsman or his son is uncertain.

### Disposition and object

> Das nachgeschriben guet zu rechten Lehen verlihen, wissentlich mit vnnd in kraft dits briefs, Was ich von Vormundtschafft wegen ... daran zu recht verleihen hab vnnd mag. Also das vorgenanter Martin ... vnnd Barthlome, bestimbts Caplers Enikhel, vnnd ire Erben, solichs Guet hinfuran innhaben, nutzen, niessen ... wie Lehens vnnd Landsrecht ist. Doch obgemelten meinem Vettern vnnd Pflegsun vnnd sunst meniglich an seinen rechten vnnd Lehen vnschedlich.

> Vnnd ist das Lehen: Nemblich Ain Ackher zu Lüentz ob der pfarr[kirchen] gelegen, der zwo ... (?) pau sind, stossend mit der andern seytten an des pfarrers Anger, auch an Johanns Schranten (or Schanten) (?), vnnd mit der obern seytten an die gemain Strassen.

The size of the field ("der zwo ... pau sind") is not read. "Ain Ackher zu Lüentz" is underlined in modern pencil, and a modern pencil note on the back reads "Lehn, einen Acker zu Lienz in Osttirol".

### Sealing and dating clause

Box `2000,2600,4400,500` ([image](https://fragmentarium.ms/loris/F-xyan/Stams_STA_Frg_200_01v.jpg/2000,2600,4400,500/full/0/default.jpg)):

> Des zu vrkundt, So hab ich für mich vnnd obgemelten meinen mitvormunder (doch mir vnnd vnser baider Erben on schaden) mein Insigel hieran gehangen. Beschehen zu Ynnsprugg am andern tag des Monats Juny, Nach Cristi vnsers lieben herrn gepurt, Funfzehenhundert vnd im funfundvierzigisten Jar.

The year word, box `2000,2650,1500,130` ([image](https://fragmentarium.ms/loris/F-xyan/Stams_STA_Frg_200_01v.jpg/2000,2650,1500,130/full/0/default.jpg)), reads "funfundvier[zigisten]", forty-fifth. The date is a plain day of the month: Innsbruck, **2 June 1545**. No feast conversion is needed.

## Physical notes

- One document of about 20 lines in German chancery cursive, with a large calligraphic initial I down the left margin and a long flourish after the dating clause. The seal clause announces a pendant seal; none is attached.
- The back carries the host volume's title, upside down relative to the deed, "1549 / Auszug der korn ausstehenden Zynns", an oval label "213", and the archive's pencil "Stams, StA, Frg. 200r". The text face carries "Frg. 200v". No early endorsement is legible.

## Catalogue corrections proposed

None has been made in Fragmentarium.

- The date is 2 June 1545, not 1525.
- The record can add the regest above: issuer Hans Trautson, as guardian with Anton von Brandis; beneficiaries Martin Wenig or Rennig and Bartlme, grandson of Hans Capler; a field at Lienz granted in fief; Innsbruck.
- The image labelled 01r is the back; the text is on 01v.

## Unresolved

- The beneficiary's surname, Wenig or Rennig.
- The ward's name, which the text does not give.
- The size of the field, a neighbour's name in the bounds, and Brandis's court office.
- Searches for Capler, Wenig and Patriasdorf in Google Books and the Internet Archive found nothing on the grant. monasterium.net refused scripted access and was not searched; the Wolkenstein family archive and the Lienz fief books were not consulted.
