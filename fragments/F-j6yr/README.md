# F-j6yr: Abbot Pelagius of Stams, with King Ferdinand's consent, sells Ludwig von Brunnenstain a redeemable yearly rent on the abbey's property, Michaelmas (29 September) [1539]

This parchment sheet in the archive of the Cistercian abbey of Stams carries one deed of sale. Pelagius, abbot of Stams ("Wir P(?)elagius aus Göttlicher verhenngnus Abbt unnd Regie[rer]"), for himself, his house and successors, sells to Ludwig von Brunnenstain a yearly rent (Gült, an annual money charge) with a right of redemption. The sale is made with the consent of the Roman royal majesty ("mit bewilligung der Römischen Kunigclichen Mayestat"), that is Ferdinand I, Roman king and lord of Tyrol. The price is in Rhenish gulden of good local currency, "ye sechtzig kreu[tzer]" (sixty kreuzer to the gulden), and the abbot acknowledges payment ("quit ledig unnd loss sagen"). As security he pledges woods, tithes and rights of the abbey "frey aige[n]", among them holdings named "[...]ungen, Viecht", followed by uncertain names. The rent is due yearly, and if it is not paid the buyer may seize the pledges "sambt Cosst unnd schaden" (with costs and damages). The abbey renounces the privileges of spiritual persons. A redemption clause names "dreuhundert gulden guetter Lanndswerung" (three hundred gulden) and provides that, once the rent is redeemed ("so die losung beschehen ist"), the letter is returned "widerumben zu unnsern hannden". The dating clause ends "sannt Michels des heiligen Ertzenngels tag" (Michaelmas, 29 September). The year stood in the lost right half of the sheet and is supplied below. The sheet was later cut down to serve as the cover of a Stams register of 1577.

The [Fragmentarium record](https://fragmentarium.ms/overview/F-j6yr) is Stams, Stiftsarchiv, Frg. 196, one parchment item, 380 x 198 mm (measured 375/380 x 182/198 mm), with the title "x" and no date, type or description. The image labelled 01r is the back and 01v the text face. Modern additions on the back are a blue-pencil "III/12", a pencil "Hs. 118", the stamp "Stiftsarchiv Stams" and the pencil shelfmarks "Stams, STA, Fag. 196r/v". The record has nothing to confirm or correct beyond the missing regest.

- **Identification:** identified, high. The regest is read on the image: the issuer (Abbot Pelagius of Stams), the royal consent, the buyer (Ludwig von Brunnenstain, named three times), the legal action (sale of a redeemable rent on pledged abbey property), the redemption sum of 300 gulden and the day of issue (Michaelmas). The first letters of the abbot's name are uncertain. The ending "-agius" is clear, and the looped capital reads as a chancery P. Pelagius Baur is the only Stams abbot of the period whose name fits: he was elected on 11 May 1525, resigned on 18 April 1540 and died on 25 November 1540 (*Monasticon metropolis Salzburgensis antiquae*, 1908). Lost in the cut right edge are the capital and the amount of the yearly rent, the saint's day of the rent, the place of issue and the year. **The year is supplied as [1539], not read.** The text names the year "funffzehenhundert unnd Neununddreissigisten" (1539) in line 7, in the clause on the price or first payment, and line 15 refers to "des vergangnen funffzehenhundert un[d ...]" ("the past fifteen hundred and ..."). The only Michaelmas in or after 1539 on which Pelagius was still abbot is 29 September 1539 (a Monday, Julian calendar). The inference fails if line 7's 1539 is a future payment date; then Michaelmas 1538 is also possible. The buyer, Ludwig von Brunnenstain, was not found in any printed or online source. The holes at the lower left are punctures, not seal slits, and the seal place falls in the lost part, so whether the sheet was sealed cannot be checked. The redemption clause would explain how a letter issued to the buyer came back to the Stams archive, but that is an inference.
- **Transcription:** the [image-only first reading](https://github.com/aaymeloglu/fragmentarium/blob/main/fragments/F-j6yr/readings/stams-2026-10-30b-image-only.json) covers all 33 lines of the text face and the back title. Its reader had no proposed identification or reading in context. The [source-assisted reading](https://github.com/aaymeloglu/fragmentarium/blob/main/fragments/F-j6yr/readings/stams-2026-10-30b-source-assisted.json) is a research reader's (Claude) reread of the whole text face at native resolution in four bands of about ten lines, with detail crops of the surname, the year and the dating line, and of the back title. It agrees with the first reading in the names and the date. It reads "Pelagius" where the first reading had "Bre?agius(?)". The holdings in line 12 ("Viecht, Bayrweiss(?), Schwanndt(?), Unnserwiecht(?)"), the first word of line 14 and line 20, which lies on a fold, remain uncertain. An unread word, "L??tz", stands before "gemelter" in line 28. Accuracy has not been measured.
- **Verification:** there is no outside text of the act to compare. A fresh reader who had not seen the reading was given neutral crops of the words the regest depends on and asked what each says. These were the abbot's name (line 1), the royal consent (line 3), the buyer's name (lines 4, 28 and 30), the year words (lines 7 and 15), the dating line (lines 32-33) and the back title. The fresh reader confirmed "funffzehenhundert vnnd Neunvnnddreÿssigisten" in line 7, "Sannt Michels des heilligen Ertzenngels tag", "Ludwigen von Brunnenstain" and "Des Gottshaus Stambs Mittelkelner Ambts Vrbar, Ad Annum 15.77". For the abbot it read only "?reagius". The coordinator reread that crop as "[W]ir Pelagius aus Gottlicher verhengnus Abbt vnnd Regi[...]", with a looped chancery P, and so the first letters are marked uncertain here. In line 15 only "funffzehenhundert vn[...]" survives; the units of that year are lost. The research reader checked the abbot's dates in the *Monasticon* (snippet) and in Siebmacher's abbot list (snippet, "Pelagius Bauer von 1525-1540"), not in page images. Passage checked independently: dating clause, canvas 1 (Stams_STA_Frg_196_01v), box 1800,3990-3780,4230, [image](https://fragmentarium.ms/loris/F-j6yr/Stams_STA_Frg_196_01v.jpg/1800,3990,1980,240/full/0/default.jpg); no outside page image. Not checked: the Tiroler Landesarchiv, where King Ferdinand's consents to alienations of monastic property may be registered, and the host volume Hs. 118. No human review.

No edition or regest of this act was found, so there is no source column. The abbot's dates come from the *Monasticon metropolis Salzburgensis antiquae* (1908), "Pelagius Baur 1491 Isny erw. 11. Mai 1525 res. 18. Apr. 1540 † 25. Nov. 1540", and from J. Siebmacher, *Wappenbuch: Bisthümer und Klöster* (1882), p. 83, "Pelagius Bauer von 1525-1540". Both were read as Google Books snippets only. The Stams abbey archive's own charter regests on monasterium.net (fonds AT-StiAStams/Urkunden) were searched for the buyer, the abbot and the years 1539 and 1540, and contain no regest of this sale. Neither do the Austrian State Archives' catalogue, the Regesta Imperii or Google Books and Internet Archive full text searched for the buyer's name.

## Physical notes and limits

- One document of 33 lines in a German chancery cursive, with a large calligraphic initial W, a figure-eight cadel in the left margin, a ruled frame running down from the text, line fillers after lines 25 and 27, and a large flourish under the last line. The right part of the sheet is cut away, losing perhaps half of every line. The left edge is torn beside lines 13-20 without loss of text.
- The back carries the cover title of the host register, box 4500,1300-6500,2150 on canvas 0 ([image](https://fragmentarium.ms/loris/F-j6yr/Stams_STA_Frg_196_01r.jpg/4500,1300,2000,850/full/0/default.jpg)): "Des Gottshaus / Stambs Mittelkel / ner Ambts Urbar, / Ad Annum 15.77." The pencil "Hs. 118" names the host volume, the Mittelkelleramt Urbar for 1577, which was not checked.
- Not claimed: the year (inferred, see above), the place of issue, the amount of the rent and of the price, the identity of Ludwig von Brunnenstain, the location of the pledged holdings, and whether the sheet is the sealed original.
