**Crop D:** manifest canvas 0, fol. [1]r column a; native region `4650,1450,1950,1500`, no rotation. Selected lines 2–8:

```text
2 introduceretur forma asini [abbreviated words unresolved here]
3 anima intellectiva infunditur a deo m(ater)ie ipsius hominis
4 in illo eodem [instanti?] m(ater)ia ipsius asini equaliter esset
5 apta ad recipiendum animam intellectivam sicut
6 m(ater)ia ipsius hominis. Quia dispositio ipsius m(ater)ie ad hoc
7 q(uod) sit nata recipere formam est denudatio ab omni alia
8 forma. …
```

These connected clauses provide better evidence than the common leprosy maxim. They agree with Hocedez's quoted argument about an ass's matter being equally ready to receive an intellective soul once stripped of every other form. A material difference remains: the manuscript appears to have **equaliter**, where Hocedez prints **sequeretur**. This must stay a reported variant, not be silently harmonized. The isolated reader's *denudata* on line 7 differs from the assisted reading *denudatio*.

The crop also shows the transition to another *impossibile* at lines 16–18, consistent with Hocedez's sequence, but its last line is clipped. No complete transcription of that next argument is claimed. The isolated reader had no candidate source, while the coordinator inspected the passage with source knowledge.
