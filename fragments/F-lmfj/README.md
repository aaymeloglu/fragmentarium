## Identification

Hippocrates, *De regimine acutorum* (Latin version from the Arabic, the Articella text), end of Book II, lemmas numbered 56–57 (recto) and 58 (verso) in the 1519 Articella, with Galen's *Commentum* in the Arabic-Latin translation. The commentary layer is Galen's, not a university commentary: every commentary line on the recto is found, in order, in the printed Galen commentary.

Editions checked:
- Articella, Venice: Philippus Pincius, 1491 (NLM copy, lemma + Galen's commentary interleaved). Full text: https://archive.org/details/101321207.nlm.nih.gov — verification passage in the OCR file `101321207_djvu.txt`, characters c. 1,206,000–1,214,000 (search "ad modum dandi cibum" or "Plurimum autem sermonis mei").
- Articella nuperrime impressa, Lyon 1519 (Hippocratic text with numbered lemmas, no commentary). Full text: https://archive.org/details/articellanuperri00gaze — lemmas 55–58 of Book II followed by "Finit liber secundus", OCR file c. char. 261,500–263,000.

Variants: the fragment's lemma reads "sanitatis ualet" for the print's "maxime agitur, ualet autem", "ad omnem accutum cognitionem" for "ad omnium accidentium cognitionem", and "ad furie quoque signe(?)" for "ad furie quoque significationem" (the print's "furie" is itself a corruption; the sense is "furiae/deliria"). "ab usu sui" in r b 2 is probably a slip for "ab usu cibi" (so r b 13). None of this affects the identification.

Position in the work: the leaf covers the last three lemmas of Book II (1519 numbering 56–58) and the beginning of the commentary on the last one; the next leaf would have carried "Finit liber secundus" and the opening of Book III ("Dulcis autem potus uel uinum...").

- Running title "REG ACUT" in red and blue across the opening.
- The verso needs raking or UV imaging; roughly half of col. b is unreadable in the current photograph.

## Ruled out

- Hippocrates *Aphorisms*/*Prognostics* with Galen, Galen's *Tegni*, *Isagoge*, Philaretus, Theophilus, Avicenna *Canon*: excluded by the running title and by the verbatim match to the Regimen acutorum.
- University commentaries (Taddeo Alderotti, Gentile da Foligno, Dino del Garbo, Jacques Despars): not needed; the commentary layer is Galen's own text as printed in 1491.
- Google Books API: no hit for the lemma wording (medieval Arabic-Latin version is poorly represented in post-1550 prints); archive.org full-text search gave no direct hit; the identification came from grepping the OCR of the 1491 and 1519 Articella volumes on archive.org. Also downloaded but not used (OCR too poor or wrong contents): Articella 1483 (BIU Santé), 1500 (BIU Santé), 1506 and 1510 (Wellcome), 1523 (Google/bub).
