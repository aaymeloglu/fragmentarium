## Evidence

The [preserved image-only response](https://github.com/aaymeloglu/fragmentarium/blob/main/fragments/F-s8db/readings/review-2026-09-22-image-only.json) has six physical rows, the last clipped, and substantial errors and gaps. Selected assisted readings, crop lines 2–5:

> l(ai)cos qui sua auct(oritat)e p(re)sumunt accipe(re) […] / eccl(es)iis q(uo)d viciu(m) p(re)cipue inficiebat oli(m) ro(manos) […] / [D]amasus (con)suluit Jeronimu(m) q(ui)d de talib(us) […] / cui ip(s)e Jero. […] ut seq(ui)t(ur) […]

Slashes indicate physical line changes. Parentheses are proposed expansions; brackets mark missing or uncertain material. The bracketed D is an assisted completion of the divided name. Hidden right-hand endings are not supplied as visible text.

The [1481 edition, Freiburg image 0415](https://dl.ub.uni-freiburg.de/diglit/guidobaysio1481/0415), printed folio 206a, lower left continuing upper right, has the same progression: laymen presuming to take church offerings, the vice formerly affecting Romans, Damasus consulting Jerome, and Jerome's ensuing response. The longer printed wording supplies *oblationes de ecclesiis* and *quid de talibus esset faciendum*. Those completions clarify the comparison; they are not a recovered manuscript reading. Orthography and abbreviation vary, and the faded response verb is left unresolved here.

An additional lower-cover crop reaches a red initial and the apparent sequence *Sanctorum … Salvatoris*. It is compatible with the next canon but does not yield enough of the commentary to verify the old extension through q.2. The upper cover is not newly collated. This additional check was candidate-aware and inconclusive, not held-out confirmation.

## Result and remaining scope

The opening supports the Rosarium identification. Retain that attribution while restricting the verified locus to the sampled c.13 commentary. The old report's c.14–q.2 c.2 endpoints, siglum expansions and full wrapper reconstruction remain unconfirmed. No-hit searches against other glosses do not establish their exclusion, and the running-commentary format alone would not identify Guido. Better images of the faded material would be needed to settle the wider span. The saved before version preserves the earlier transcription and reconstruction.
