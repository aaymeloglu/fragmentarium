# F-fc4z: Emperor Rudolf II grants arms with the Lehenartikel to Georg Jäger, father of Abbot Melchior of Stams, Prague, 18 June 1601 (unfinished exemplar)

This parchment sheet in the archive of the Cistercian abbey of Stams is an unfinished exemplar of an imperial grant of arms. Emperor Rudolf II grants Georg Jäger, whose son Melchior is the reigning abbot of Stams, together with Georg's legitimate heirs and their heirs forever, a coat of arms with the Lehenartikel (the clause admitting the bearers to the status of persons capable of holding fiefs). The arms: a narrow bend of white and red lozenges set "Sachsenweiss"; a tilting helm with mantling blue and white on the left and red and white on the right; as crest a white greyhound with a gilded collar, springing. The act is dated at the royal castle of Prague, in the 26th year of Rudolf's Roman reign; 18 June 1601 by the register of grants.

The same grant survives at Stams on a second unfinished sheet in another hand and wording, [F-pqjj](https://fragmentarium.ms/overview/F-pqjj) (Frg. 201). The two records carry one act.

The [Fragmentarium record](https://fragmentarium.ms/overview/F-fc4z) is Stams, Stiftsarchiv, Frg. 202, catalogued as "Deed", German, Prague, 1601, 366 x 251 mm, removed from the Urbar STB III/30.

- **Identification:** identified, high. Issuer, beneficiary, his relationship to the abbot, the grant of arms with the Lehenartikel, the place (Prague) and the Roman regnal year are read on the image. The day and month are uncertain on the sheet, and the year in words is cut off; the regnal year gives 1601, and the day, 18 June, is supplied from K. F. von Frank, *Standeserhebungen und Gnadenakte*, vol. 2 (1970), whose entry agrees with the sheet on every element it gives: "Jäger, Georg, Sohn: Melchior, Abt zu Stams in Tirol, Wappen m. Lehenart., Prag 18. VI. 1601, (R)".
- **Transcription:** source-assisted, selected passages: the display line and protocol, the narration naming the parties, the blazon, the penalty and reservation clauses, and the dating clause. The Lehenartikel was read in outline only. A research reader (Claude) made the reading at native resolution from contrast-enhanced crops. Three further Claude readers then read the sheet from the images alone, with neutral questions and no proposed answer; they worked after the identification, so they are not first readings in the sense of CONVENTIONS and are not preserved in `readings/`. Every disagreement among them was re-read at native resolution, and the readings below give the result, with the remaining uncertainty marked. Accuracy has not been measured. No human review.
- **Verification:** a research reader (Claude) compared the sheet's beneficiary, relationship clause, act, place and date with Frank's entry, seen as a Google Books search snippet (volume id `s-dMAQAAIAAJ`); the page number was not established. The Reichsadelsakten file in Vienna that Frank's "(R)" points to was not found online. The dating clause was checked on the image by a second Claude reader. No human review.

The line ends to the right run under modern tape or off the trimmed edge, so the field division and any charges beside the bend are not complete on this sheet. The text is set around a blank field right of centre (about `5300,2150,1250,1200`) left for the painted arms that the blazon refers to; it was never painted.

### Reservation and dating clause

Box `2600,3900,3400,450` ([image](https://fragmentarium.ms/loris/F-fc4z/Stams_STA_Frg_202_01v.jpg/2600,3900,3400,450/full/0/default.jpg)).

> [...] an Iren Wappen vnnd Rechten vnvergriffen vnnd vnschädlich. Mit Vrkhundt dis [briefs ...] Geben ist auf vnserm Khüniglichen Schloss zu Prag, den [a]chtzehenden (or [vi]ertzehenden) tag des Monats Junij (or Julij), Nach Christi vnsers li[eben Herrn ...] [...] des Römischen im Sechsundzwainzigisten, des H[ungerischen im ...]undzwainzigisten, vnnd des Behaimischen auch im Se[...]

"Khüniglichen Schloss zu Prag" and "Römischen im Sechsundzwainzigisten" are secure. The first letters of the day word sit on a glossy crease: the reading is "achtzehenden" (18th) or "viertzehenden" (14th). The month is "Junij" or "Julij". The year in words is lost at the trimmed right edge; the first element of the Hungarian year is illegible and the Bohemian year is cut off.

### Dating

Rudolf II was elected King of the Romans on 27 October 1575, and his chancery counted the Roman regnal years from the election, so the 26th Roman year ran from 27 October 1600 to 26 October 1601. A June or July in that year is 1601. The Hungarian year's surviving "...undzwainzigisten" agrees with the 29th year (from the coronation of 25 September 1572, 25 September 1600 to 24 September 1601) but its first element is not read. The day is supplied from Frank: 18 June 1601. The reading "achtzehenden ... Junij" fits that date; "viertzehenden" and "Julij" are not excluded on the image. The date is a day of the month, not a feast, so no feast conversion is needed.

## Physical notes and catalogue

- Neither this sheet nor F-pqjj is the engrossment handed to the beneficiary. Both leave the field for the painted arms blank, both have struck-out words (here an interlinear word above "vnnd vnschädlich" in the reservation clause), and neither has a plica, seal or signature; the lower edge is cut through the regnal-year clause. The two texts are different redactions in different hands, not copies of one another. Drafts or rejected fair copies that stayed at Stams are the likely explanation; nothing on the sheets documents it.
- The hand is a rapid German chancery cursive below large Fraktur display letters.
- The back carries the host volume's cover title in a 17th-century hand, "Vrbarium. | Weltkheller(?) Ambts auf | das Sechzehenhundert zwelfft(?) Jar", the shelf label III/30, and tie slits along the spine fold. F-pqjj's back carries a matching title for 1612, so the two sheets were probably reused as covers for the same set of Urbare.
- Proposed catalogue corrections (Fragmentarium not updated): the document is an unfinished exemplar of an imperial grant of arms with the Lehenartikel for Georg Jäger, 18 June 1601; the image labelled 01r is the back of the sheet and the text is on 01v.

## Limits

- Frank was seen only as a search snippet; the page was not seen and the Vienna file was not consulted.
- The Lehenartikel and much of the body were read in outline, not word by word.
- The day and month rest on Frank; the sheet's own day word and month are each read in two ways.
- No palaeographer has reviewed the readings.
