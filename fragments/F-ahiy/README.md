- Catalogue: "Unidentified encyclopaedia", lemmata modestia, Moyses, mollis, monachus, morbus, mors; 1351–1400; Northern Textualis; Latin; parchment; 1 leaf (part), 285 x 199 mm.

## Status

PARTIAL. Genre and every quoted source are established, but the compilation itself is not identified. It is NOT a lexicon (Catholicon, Papias, Hugutio, Brito) and NOT a biblical Distinctiones: it is an alphabetical *tabula/florilegium of auctoritates* that gives short quotations and "Quod…" content summaries with references to the source by work, book/epistle, chapter and pericope letter (a–g). Sources cited on the two sides: Ambrose De officiis I and III; Cicero De inventione ("2 Rethorice"); Cassiodorus (Variae x.15); Valerius Maximus; Moralium dogma philosophorum ("liber de dogmate philosophorum"); Aristotle Ethica (VII, "Mollis"), De animalibus 16 and 20, Meteora 2; Augustine Contra Faustum 22, De civitate Dei 3, 13, 16; Jerome epistles ad Damasum, ad Eustochium (22), ad Heliodorum (60), ad Pammachium, ad Domnionem (50), ad Rusticum; pseudo-Dionysius De ecclesiastica hierarchia 6; Richard of St Victor De XII patriarchis; pseudo-Anselm De similitudinibus; a "liber de perfectione evangelica"; Seneca Epistulae ad Lucilium 19, 41, 44, De beneficiis, De remediis fortuitorum, Proverbia; Gregory Moralia; Glossa on Ephesians and John. This is the citation profile of a late-13th/14th-century alphabetical tabula of originalia (Kilwardby/Franciscan "tabula" style), not of Bartholomaeus, Vincent, Bersuire or the Manipulus florum.

Conclusion on genre: an alphabetical tabula of auctoritates keyed to *originalia* with chapter and pericope-letter references (a–g). The Jerome letters are numbered by a medieval collection order ("ad Damasum 16 ep. 15 e", "ad Eustochium 6 ep. 28", "ad Heliodorum 10 ep. 12 g", "ad Pammachium 11 ep. 6 a", "ad Domnionem 3 b"), which would be a strong fingerprint for the compilation once a candidate is in hand.

## Ruled out (checked)

- Johannes Balbus, Catholicon; Papias, Elementarium; Hugutio, Derivationes; Guillelmus Brito, Summa: ruled out on content. The fragment has no etymologies, derivations or grammatical definitions; entries are strings of patristic/classical quotations and "Quod…" summaries with book/chapter/letter references. The lemma sequence Modestia–Moyses–Mollis–Monachus–Mons–Monstra–Mora–Morbus–Mors also includes persons and things a derivational lexicon treats differently.
- Distinctiones (Alan of Lille PL 210, Maurice of Provins, Nicolas de Byard): ruled out on content, no biblical-sense distinctions; the fragment quotes Aristotle, Seneca, Valerius Maximus, Cassiodorus.
- Bartholomaeus Anglicus: not alphabetical (per brief), not checked.
- Manipulus florum (Thomas of Ireland): the citation style (chapter + letter a–g) does not match Manipulus practice, and Manipulus has no person/thing lemmata such as Moyses or Monstra. The manipulusflorum.com lemma list could not be fetched (empty response), so this exclusion rests on the citation style only.
