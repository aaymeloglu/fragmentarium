## Status

**IDENTIFIED.** Nicholas of Lyra, *Tractatus de differentia nostrae translationis ab hebraica littera Veteris Testamenti* (1333; Stegmüller, Repertorium Biblicum no. 5977), Genesis section. Front pastedown = Gen 2:18 – 6:16; back pastedown = Gen 16:13 – 28:18. Verbatim against the Rouen 1507 edition (Martin Morin), Bibliothèque Sainte-Geneviève copy OEXV 848 RES on archive.org, ff. ii v – iii r (front) and vii r – vii v (back). Not the Postilla litteralis: the Postilla text on the same verses (Koberger 1487, archive.org biblia-sacra-lyra_202308) is a much longer, differently worded treatment; the fragment has Lyra's own condensed Hebrew-vs-Vulgate handbook, in its characteristic format (chapter number, concordance letter, Vulgate lemma, "Hebre." + Hebrew reading, short comment), announced in the tract's prologue ("signabo capitula per ordinem ... loca vero capitulorum signabo per litteras, sicut in concordanciis fieri consuevit. Ponam etiam primo litteram translationis nostre et litteram hebraicam", Gloss-e ed. of the prologue, see link below).

## Notes / queries

- Catalogue title "unidentified commentary on the Book of Genesis" can be replaced by: Nicolaus de Lyra, Tractatus de differentia nostrae translationis ab hebraica littera (Stegmüller RB 5977), Genesis 2:18–6:16 and 16:13–28:18. Date 1375–1400 is consistent (work composed 1333). The tract is short (Genesis occupies c. 8 folios in the 1507 quarto); the two pastedowns are two non-adjacent leaves of one large-format copy, with at least one leaf (Gen 6:16–16:13) lost between them.
- The sketch at the foot of front col. b illustrates the ark's three storeys ("cenacula et tristega", Gen 6:16) and appears to be the "prout hic figuratur" diagram of the tract itself, so it may be authorial or archetypal; worth checking against BnF lat. 3359.
