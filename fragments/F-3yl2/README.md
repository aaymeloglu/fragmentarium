### Conclusion

- The catalogue's "Summa theologiae IIa-IIae q. 10 a. 11" is correct for the source of leaf 1v, but the leaf is not a copy of the Summa: lines 12-13 and 19 are foreign, and leaf 2r condenses a. 12 into ~10 lines, adds Roman-law and Decretum references, then passes to q. 11 a. 1 with a rubric ("Restat videndum de heresi") and a canon reference in the form used by canonists ("xxiiij q. iij Heresis").
- Genre: a compendium/abbreviatio of the Secunda secundae (de fide / de infidelitate / de heresi) by a compiler trained in both laws, i.e. of the type of the early 14th-century Summae for confessors that digest Aquinas with utriusque iuris citations (Johannes de Friburgo, Summa confessorum, 1298; Astesanus, Summa de casibus, 1317; Bartholomaeus de S. Concordio, Summa Pisana, 1338) or an anonymous "abbreviatio secundae secundae". Not the Speculum morale (which copies Aquinas verbatim).
- Dating: post-1272 (Secunda secundae); 14th c. is consistent. The layout (single wide column, ~19 widely spaced lines, cursiva antiquior) suggests a working/study copy rather than a library Summa.
