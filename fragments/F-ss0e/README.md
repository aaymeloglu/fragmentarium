The common *turpe est doctori* proverb alone would not identify a collection. The manuscript's word order around *docet alios* is not an exact continuous match as currently read, and most printed words fall outside the surviving strip. They must remain on the print side of the comparison. The possible *optimum/optimus* ending is unresolved.

The middle crop provides a further manuscript check: *2° p(ro)batur … / … p(ro)phetica. Nam … / …rtimini ad me / …uertar ad vos*, followed by fragments of *… impius viam suam* and *… ad d(omi)n(u)m qui misere…*. These support a sequence of prophetic proofs, but the corresponding complete printed continuation was unavailable. This is a content check, not independent confirmation of Cherubino's collection.

## Limits

Keep the substantial parallel and the unresolved compiler distinct. A later compilation can preserve earlier material; its publication date does not settle the fragment's date or the direction of borrowing. The catalogue's date/script assessment requires specialist review before any replacement is proposed. Side r and the complete sermon were not newly collated. Earlier corpus no-hits are not exclusions. A full page and adjacent sermon text would allow a longer comparison; the missing evidence is explicit rather than filled from the source.
