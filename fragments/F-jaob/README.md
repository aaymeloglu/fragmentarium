# F-jaob — anonymous Sentences IV epitome with mnemonic verses

Re-adjudicated 22 September 2026.

- **Identification: partial, medium confidence.** The sampled verse block corresponds to the pseudo-Bonaventure *Sententiae Sententiarum*. A prose list parallels the summaries printed in Jan Hus's *Super IV Sententiarum*. Neither connection identifies the compiler of this combined text or establishes the direction of borrowing.
- **Transcription: selected source-assisted readings.** The isolated reading covered five physical lines of fol.70r. The coordinator inspected that band and additional fol.70r/71r bands. Abbreviations and uncertain wording remain unresolved; the old broad transcription is not certified.
- **Verification: manuscript images against edition OCR.** The prose list was compared with Flajšhans p.593 and the distinct verse block with Peltier VI p.655. Those edition passages were read in cached OCR, not newly inspected printed images. No human palaeographic review or numerical accuracy assessment is claimed.

[Catalogue](https://fragmentarium.ms/overview/F-jaob): Paris, BnF, Latin9377, ff.70–73, catalogued as fourteenth-century French Cursiva. [Manifest](https://fragmentarium.ms/metadata/iiif/F-jaob/manifest.json); [Gallica fol.71r, image f153](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b525133302/f153.item). The reading bands retain cached pixel positions in their labels, but their complete original coordinate transforms were not recovered. No new enhancement was applied.

## Prose sample: flagella

The [preserved isolated response](https://github.com/aaymeloglu/fragmentarium/blob/main/fragments/F-jaob/readings/review-2026-09-22-image-only.json) is an uncertain five-line reading of the fol.70r band labelled r70_1550. The assisted comparison uses the visible sequence, not a reconstruction of every word. The following normalized text expands abbreviations without claiming that every expansion boundary has been resolved:

```text
2 … per patientiam augeantur
3 … ad custodiam virtutum ne superbia … ut Paulo … ad cor-
4 rigenda peccata … ad dei gloriam … de ceco nato … ad initium
5 pene ut Herodi …
```

These are selected portions of crop lines 2–5, with omitted text marked. A subsequent source-aware image check of the cached r70_1550 band confirmed that line range and the physical *cor / rigenda* division. The hyphen above is an editorial word-division marker. The linked image-only response retains such forms as *custodiā (custodiam)* and *virtutū? (virtutum?)*; the normalized quotation does not certify unresolved abbreviation details. The [Hus edition, BookIV d.15, p.593](https://archive.org/details/superivsententia41husj_0) enumerates five purposes of punishment in that order: increased merit (Job), preservation of virtue (Paul), correction of sin (Miriam's leprosy), God's glory (the man born blind), and punishment's beginning (Herod). The sustained list is a useful parallel; both texts summarize Lombard, so it does not establish a distinctive Hus attribution.

The old expansion *Quatuor modis* conflicts with its own five-item list. This review does not retain it as a secure reading. The cropped opening's numeral/abbreviation is uncertain; the edition's *quinque* is comparative evidence, not permission to write that word into the manuscript. The form of the Marian/leprosy example also needs a clearer reading before choosing between *Marie lepra* and the old *Marie leprose*.

## Additional check: verse layer

Fol.71r's band r71_2240 shows a two-column verse block. Selected assisted reading of the left block, the first seven complete verse rows below the clipped preceding text:

> Vir mulierq(ue) pares s(un)t iur(is) redditio(n)e / In cunctis aliis est vir caput ex ratione / Si contradicat in voto vir mulieri / Sive viro mulier p(ro) nullo debet haberi / Ad ius reddendum dicas utrumq(ue) teneri / Concedente viro si coniunx polliceatur / Hinc habitu(m) mutet votu(m) nunquam revocatur.

The [Peltier edition, pseudo-Bonaventure, volumeVI p.655, distinctionXXXII](https://archive.org/details/srecardinalissbo06bona) has this sequence. OCR errors such as *mulieique* and fused *jurisredditioue* are treated as OCR, not manuscript variants. The manuscript's right-hand continuation is more difficult and is not silently filled from Peltier. A separate fol.70r band r70_3450 shows the transition into distinction16 below a verse block, confirming the alternating verse/prose format locally. These are additional candidate-aware checks, not held-out evidence.

## Result

Retain the anonymous epitome and two textual layers. Do not call the entire fragment a work by Hus or Bonaventure. The prose list and verse sequence were checked in different passages, but the remaining leaves, broad endpoints, and complete verse variants were not newly collated. Earlier exclusions based only on commentary format or unsuccessful searches are not retained. A full printed-image comparison and study of other epitomes could identify the compiling work; nothing here independently establishes a recension, dating revision or direction of dependence.
