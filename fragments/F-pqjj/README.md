# F-pqjj: Emperor Rudolf II grants arms with the Lehenartikel to Georg Jäger, father of the abbot of Stams, 1601 (unfinished exemplar)

This parchment sheet in the archive of the Cistercian abbey of Stams is an unfinished exemplar of an imperial grant of arms. Emperor Rudolf II grants Georg Jäger, as father of the reigning abbot of Stams, together with his legitimate heirs and their heirs forever, a coat of arms with the Lehenartikel (the clause admitting the bearers to the status of persons capable of holding fiefs). The arms: a narrow bend of white and red lozenges set "Sachsweis"; a tilting helm with mantling blue and white on the left and red and white on the right; as crest a white greyhound with a gilded collar, springing. The dating clause gives the 26th year of Rudolf's Roman reign and, probably, the year 1601; the register of grants dates the act Prague, 18 June 1601.

The same grant survives at Stams on a second unfinished sheet in another hand and wording, [F-fc4z](https://fragmentarium.ms/overview/F-fc4z) (Frg. 202), which keeps the place and a reading of the day. The two records carry one act.

The [Fragmentarium record](https://fragmentarium.ms/overview/F-pqjj) is Stams, Stiftsarchiv, Frg. 201, catalogued as "Deed", German, Prague, 1601, 361 x 291 mm, removed from the Urbar STB VIII/21.

- **Identification:** identified, high. The imperial titles, the beneficiary Georg Jäger, the relationship to the reigning abbot of Stams, the grant of arms with the Lehenartikel, the blazon, and the 26th Roman regnal year are read on the image. The issuer's name in the display line is lost; the titles and regnal year make him Rudolf II. The year in words is probable ("[...]hundert vnnd im Erst[en]"); place, day and month are illegible and are supplied from K. F. von Frank, *Standeserhebungen und Gnadenakte*, vol. 2 (1970): "Jäger, Georg, Sohn: Melchior, Abt zu Stams in Tirol, Wappen m. Lehenart., Prag 18. VI. 1601, (R)". The blazon and parties agree with F-fc4z, which names the son Melchior and the place.
- **Transcription:** source-assisted, selected passages: the protocol, the narration, the blazon, part of the Lehenartikel, the penalty clause and the dating clause. A research reader (Claude) made the reading at native resolution from contrast-enhanced crops, using the blue channel with histogram equalisation for the faded last lines. Three further Claude readers then read the sheet from the images alone, with neutral questions and no proposed answer; they worked after the identification, so they are not first readings in the sense of CONVENTIONS and are not preserved in `readings/`. Every disagreement among them was re-read at native resolution, and the readings below give the result, with the remaining uncertainty marked. Accuracy has not been measured. No human review.
- **Verification:** a research reader (Claude) compared the beneficiary, relationship, act and year with Frank's entry, seen as a Google Books search snippet (volume id `s-dMAQAAIAAJ`); the page number was not established. The blazon and parties were compared with F-fc4z on its images. The Reichsadelsakten file in Vienna was not found online. No human review.

"vnnserm Künig[c]lichen" and "Römischen im Sechsundzwainzig[isten]" are secure. The place name is not visible, and the day and month are illegible in the faded, water-stained line. The year in words is probable only: "Se" at the start and "...dert vnnd im Er..." are visible, the middle letters faded. The Hungarian and Bohemian years are lost at the cut lower edge.

### Dating

Rudolf II was elected King of the Romans on 27 October 1575, and his chancery counted the Roman regnal years from the election, so the 26th Roman year ran from 27 October 1600 to 26 October 1601. The probable year in words, 1601, falls in it. Place, day and month are supplied from Frank: Prague, 18 June 1601. F-fc4z, the other exemplar, reads Prague and a day and month consistent with that date.

## Physical notes and catalogue

- Neither this sheet nor F-fc4z is the engrossment handed to the beneficiary. Both leave the field for the painted arms blank, both have struck-out words (here in the corroboration area, about `3800,3560,600,50`, with an interlinear correction), and neither has a plica, seal or signature; the lower edge is cut through the regnal-year line. Drafts or rejected fair copies that stayed at Stams are the likely explanation; nothing on the sheets documents it.
- The hand is a formal upright chancery script, not the cursive of F-fc4z.
- The back carries the host volume's cover title, "[Vrba]r(?) | [...]hs Ambts auf | Anno 1.6.12. Jar", the shelf label VIII/21, and tie slits along the spine fold like those on F-fc4z, whose cover title also names 1612. The two sheets were probably reused as covers for the same set of Urbare.
- Proposed catalogue corrections (Fragmentarium not updated): the document is an unfinished exemplar of an imperial grant of arms with the Lehenartikel for Georg Jäger, 1601 (18 June by Frank); the image labelled 01r is the back of the sheet and the text is on 01v.
